# Resistances

## How Resistance Grows

Resistance unlocks at **Skyborn tier (Level 5)** and grows gradually from there, rather than switching on all at once at the top of the Power Scale.

**Level 5 — first Resistance.** You gain Resistance to your own bonded element. Any roll made against you using that element takes a **−1** penalty.

**Level 7 — Improved Resistance.** The penalty deepens to **−2**, and you may choose one additional element — one you've genuinely encountered in your journeys, not just any element on the table — to resist at that same −2 penalty.

**Level 9 — Improved Resistance.** The penalty deepens again to **−3**, and you may choose another encountered element to add to your resisted list, also at −3.

*(These −1/−2/−3 step values are the natural reading of "an increased reduction... as you gain levels," matched to the Level-Up Baseline's milestone markers at 5/7/9 — flag if the actual intended progression differs. Also worth confirming: the phrase describing this growth mentions "a reduction... imposed onto others," which could mean something distinct from the incoming-roll penalty above — as written here, both are treated as the same single mechanic, just growing in magnitude and element count.)*

**Sovereign tier (9–10)** no longer introduces Resistance for the first time — by this point you've already been carrying it since Level 5. What's new at Sovereign is **Metaphysical Weight** (Ch4 §3), arriving alongside your second Resistance improvement.

## Why Your Native Element First

Your bonded element is the one you have the deepest, most practiced relationship with. Starting Resistance there, then expanding outward to elements you've actually crossed paths with in play, keeps the mechanic tied to lived character history rather than a flat, generic "gets tougher" number.

## Not Full Immunity

Even at its highest tier, Resistance is a penalty applied to incoming rolls — not a wall. A resisted element's Uses still land; they just have to work harder to do it.
