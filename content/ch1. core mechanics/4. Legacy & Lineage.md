# Legacy & Lineage

Everything your character can do - every ability, every elemental tie, every principle they hold to - belongs to your Legacy. If you find yourself creating a clan, building your legacy from an existing sects framework or another standing group then rather than having a Legacy, you will instead hold or be part of a Lineage.
## Legacy

Your **Legacy** is your characters story carved into stone: The Practitioner building blocks, your elemental ties, the tenets and morals you hold, and whatever cultural significance your Legacy carries.

Your Legacy isn't handed to you as a finished package. It grows as you play - starting from the baseline actions and Practitioner type every character shares (See Ch3. p2), then shaped further by the Identity (See Ch2. p7) you choose once you reach Kindled tier. 
An Identity does not define your character completely. Two characters can pick the same Identity and still end up with completely different Legacies because Identity shapes *how* your kit behaves, not the full content of who you are.

A Legacy can be built from a single Ability type alone,(See Ch3. p1) or spread evenly across all four: Burst, Creation, Harmony & Sense.

## Lineage

A **Lineage** is the same structure at cultural scale. When enough people converge on the same, or a closely related, Legacy; through shared teaching, shared origin, or shared circumstance - one person's individual path becomes a living tradition passed onto others.

This is the mechanism behind how a whole culture ends up with its own flavor of magic: the Elemental Suite's underlying physics (See Ch4. p5 for a table of the elements) is universal, but a Lineage is the cultural lens a whole people looks at this through.
## Building Your Character Around your Legacy

Keep Legacy and Lineage in mind as two different questions when you build your character:

- **Legacy** - what can *you* do, and why do you do it that way?
- **Lineage** - did you learn this from somewhere? Is there a tradition, a people, or a teacher behind your path - or are you forging something nobody's quite done this way before?

Neither answer needs to be dramatic. A quiet, personally-taught Legacy is just as honest as one passed down through a centuries-old Lineage.
Worth noting is that a Lineage is mainly a flavor tie in to the world, it helps you build your background, who you know, where your allegiance lies and who you consider an enemy to be. You can choose this as your character grows if you wish to build a Lineage or you can play a full campaign without touching it. It's your character, it's your choice!
