# The Sprite / Boundless / Prime Elemental

Every element in the world follows the same three-stage hierarchy, uniform in shape no matter which element it is.

| Stage                   | What It Is                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| ----------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Sprite**              | The smallest, weakest manifestation of an element. This is the stage tied to Sprite Bonding [[8. Sprite Bonding]]).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| **Boundless Elemental** | This is the model of Medium Tier ELemental an Ascendant practitioner starts to resemble as their Metaphysical Weight ([[3. Birth and Death of the Elements]]) grows.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| **Prime Elemental**     | The apex stage of Elemental growth. Not quite to the presence and strength of a Prime Elemental, though definitely able to hold one off if it enters Reality and ravages the land. The makeup of a Prime Elemental effects both the Planar and Reality levels of the world, as a result the world simply knows when one is near and manifests its element in a nexus as a signature of that presence. |
## What Scholars Still Debate
Beneath this hierarchy sit several questions no scholar of the Elemental Suite has fully settled:

- Whether scale and intensity are the *only* thing separating a Sprite from a Boundless Elemental from a Prime, or whether some deeper structural difference divides the three stages.
	- Some posit that Primes are the first of their kind, born at the birth of their paired Element.
- Whether every element passes through all three stages in turn, or whether some, Bound and Planar elements chief among the suspects, skip a stage entirely.
- How two different elements' Sprites, Boundless Elementals, or Primes behave toward one another when their domains happen to overlap.
- Whether a newly Born element ([[2. Elemental Plane]]) always begins at Sprite stage and grows from there, or can enter the world directly at a larger scale.
These are the kind of open questions a researchers *"Reach for the Elements"* check might one day chip away at.
