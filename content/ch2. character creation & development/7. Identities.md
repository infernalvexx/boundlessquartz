# Identities

Once you reach **Kindled tier (Level 3)**, it's time to give your character a signature. Before that point, you're building your Legacy's first abilities out of the shared baseline Building Blocks (Ch3 §1) and getting comfortable with your character sheet — Identities are deliberately gated behind Level 3 so they don't overwhelm you before you've found your footing.

## Choosing a Signature

Look through the list below and decide on the signature expression and style of character you want to grow into. This list is only a taste of what's possible in Boundless — pick one outright, or bring an idea to your GM and work out something bespoke together. If a homebrewed signature doesn't end up feeling fun in play, you're always free to try something else, or fall back on one of the list below.

| Identity | Unique Resource/Mechanic | Identity Goal | Seen on |
|---|---|---|---|
| Feed your blade with magic to devastating effect | **Charge Counters** — bank charge *on the weapon itself* to unleash whenever you please | Charge the blade through casting, cash it in through melee. | Corvin Ashe (L3), Thane Rourke (L10) |
| Use your blood to defend your party | **Spend Blood Pips Directly** — Resource Protection is inverted | Be the one taking the hits. | Petra Vane (L3) |
| Not getting hit is the key to success | **Focus** — build Focus through being targeted by your enemies | Delay melee for as long as possible, punish those who chase you. | Nyx Cadence (L6) |
| More Exhaustion? More Power! | **Turn Blood Pips into Verve Pips** as you please | Trade blows to come out on top. | Wren Halloway (L7) |
| Cultivate a bond with your element, raise a companion | **Elemental Construct** — gain a tactical advantage through bonus resources | Field and puppet a semi-independent second combatant. | Ilyria Vask (L9) |
| Keep away from enemies until you're ready to engage | **Patience** — each action not spent harming an opponent builds Patience | Unleash your Patient Hammer to devastating effect, or stand strong in your Patient Shield. | Dashiell Or (L5) |
| Awareness of your surroundings keeps everyone alive | **Transfuse** — hitting clean steals a pip which you can give to an ally | Target high-value enemies, restore in-need allies. | Sable Quill (L4) |
| Getting hurt is the plan | Less Blood Pips gives positive yet detrimental bonuses | Challenge death to unleash the pinnacle of your strength. | Baldric Fenn (L8) |
| Intrinsically link yourself to another | **Bond** — share resources with your chosen partner | Provide utility and support in an unparalleled manner. | Cass Aurelian (L9) |

*Corvin and Thane share this Identity, and yet the same signature mechanic reads completely differently between them — a Kindled-tier Shaper still finding his footing, against a Sovereign-tier Wielder who has spent a lifetime mastering his. The Identity names the mechanic; the character wielding it supplies everything else.*

## Why This Comes After Building Blocks, Not Before

Identity is a specialization layer on top of your Legacy, not a replacement for it. Your Building Blocks and your Skills/Approaches already define what your character can fundamentally do; Identity gives that foundation a signature hook — a resource, a rhythm, a reason your kit feels like *yours* and not just a generic list of abilities.
