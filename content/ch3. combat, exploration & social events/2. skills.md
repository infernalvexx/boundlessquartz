# Skills

Nine Skills form the backbone of what your character is competent at:

| Skill | Skill | Skill |
|---|---|---|
| Fortitude | Instinct | Attunement |
| Grit | Force | Insight |
| Focus | Charisma | Craft |

## How Skills Work

At character creation, you assign each of the nine Skills to one of the three Resource Pools — Blood, Might, or Verve. This assignment is permanent, and it decides which Pool you roll whenever you use that Skill. The assignment itself grants no numeric bonus — it only decides which dice you reach for.

Skills answer "what are you doing" — the raw category of action, independent of style.

*Assign Grit to Might, and every time you lean on Grit — bracing against a blow, holding a door shut against a battering ram — you roll your Might Pool. Assign it to Verve instead, and the same Skill draws on your Verve dice instead; the Skill's name doesn't change, only which Pool answers when you reach for it.*

## Why Skills Stay Fixed

Skills are your character's stable identity axis. Unlike your Legacy or Identity, which can grow and shift, your Skills — and which Pool each rolls — never change once set at creation. Two characters can have wildly different Legacies and still share every Skill-to-Pool assignment; two characters can also make the exact opposite choices and end up rolling completely different Pools for the same nominal Skill. Either way, this axis of your character stays put, giving you something dependable to build the rest of your identity around.

## Favored Skills

Once you've assigned all nine Skills to a Pool and chosen your Practitioner Type (Ch2 §2), pick **2 of your 9 Skills to Favor.**

This is deliberately the last step in that order. By the time you pick, you already know which of your Skills landed in a Pool that won't see as much use compared to your Practitioner Type's stronger Pool. Favoring a Skill in that weaker Pool gives it real teeth even though the Pool it rolls from is a smaller part of your build — pure character flavor, made mechanically worth it. Favoring a Skill in your *dominant* Pool instead just makes you even more dangerous in the area you already lean into — the min-max path, and just as legitimate a choice as the flavor one.

**Mechanical effect:** whenever you roll a check using a Favored Skill, you may **spend 1 Pip** (from any Pool) to add your current **Favored Skill Bonus** directly to the roll's total. Not passive — it's an active choice each time, gated by a resource you already track. That bonus grows with Level — see the Level-Up Baseline (Ch2 §1) for the current numbers.

*(This is deliberately Pip-gated rather than always-on, specifically so it doesn't silently compound with Pool Roll Bonus, Ch2 §1/§2, on the same roll — a Pip-poor character can't double-dip every action. See `Brainstorm/Level-Up Baseline Progression.md` for the comparison that led here.)*

*(Current assumption: both of your Favored Skills share the same single Level-based bonus number and the same Pip cost, rather than each accruing its own separate track — flag if that's not the intent.)*
