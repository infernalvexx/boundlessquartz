# Practitioners: Cast / Wield / Shape

Every practitioner of magic falls into one of three types, set by which Resource Pool (Ch2 §3) they lean on. This triad is your character's flavor layer for *how* they express power — caster, martial, or a hybrid of the two — independent of which literal Pool your individual Skills happen to be assigned to.

| Type | Lean | Default Speed |
|---|---|---|
| **Cast / Caster** | Verve-heavy | 2 |
| **Wield / Wielder** | Might-heavy | 3 |
| **Shape / Shaper** | Verve and Might roughly equal | 3 |

Blood sits outside this axis entirely — it's your survival and health, not a "how you act" descriptor, so it plays no part in deciding whether you're a Caster, Wielder, or Shaper.

## Why It Matters Beyond Flavor

Practitioner Type decides which Pool you roll for your Speed check at the top of combat (Ch3 §4):

- **Casters** roll Verve.
- **Wielders** roll Might.
- **Shapers choose** which of the two to roll, fresh each encounter.

That choice is the trade-off for sitting in the middle: a Shaper gives up a guaranteed higher starting Speed or pool pip bonus in exchange for rolling whichever Pool serves them best for each encounter.
