# Power Scale
Your character's growth maps directly onto a single Level 1–10 track, split into five tiers of two levels each.

| Tier | Levels | Concept | Flight / Combat Band | Seen on |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Nascent** | 1–2 | Foundational, ground-level elemental interaction | Ground only | Ren Talbot, Yuki Marrow |
| **Kindled** | 3–4 | Basic combat training | Ground only | Corvin Ashe, Petra Vane, Sable Quill |
| **Skyborn** | 5–6 | Moderate combat training; newly welcomed to the sky | Ground + Low Sky | Dashiell Or, Nyx Cadence |
| **Ascendant** | 7–8 | Deep combat training; specialization begins | Ground + Low Sky + High Sky | Wren Halloway, Baldric Fenn |
| **Sovereign** | 9–10 | True mastery; multiple specializations | All bands, general elemental resistance | Ilyria Vask, Cass Aurelian, Thane Rourke |

## What Each Tier Unlocks

- **Nascent (1–2):** you're learning the baseline actions and Building Blocks everyone starts with ([[1. Abilities]]) and finding your footing with your character sheet.
- **Kindled (3–4):** this is where you choose a signature **Identity** ([[7. Identities]]), the point the game trusts you to know your character well enough to specialise.
- **Skyborn (5–6):** Access to the Low Sky flight band opens up ([[8. Flight Tools]]), the first tier where vertical traversal becomes a tactical option. This is also where **Resistance first unlocks** ([[9. Resistances]]), starting with your own bonded element.
- **Ascendant (7–8):** The High Sky band becomes accessible, your Legacy's specialisation deepens further, and your Resistance improves ([[9. Resistances]])).
- **Sovereign (9–10):** The final steps to true mastery: every Flight Band is open to you, your Resistance improves further and you begin to carry a passive Metaphysical Weight within the world, dragging your element whereever you go ([[3. Birth and Death of the Elements]]).

Your Pip totals are tied to the choices you make building your Legacy at creation instead of a flat formula. How you've built your character heavily impacts your resource totals.
