# GM Scene Die

The GM Scene Die is a pacing tool your GM banks over the course of a scene and spends at their own discretion — to introduce a complication, a twist, or a moment of real pressure that keeps the scene alive.

## Earning It

A Scene Die is banked whenever a roll comes up with **doubles or matching dice** - two or more dice landing on the same value in a single roll. This echoes the same matching-dice idea that governs Crisis (Ch3 §6), so the table only has to remember one shared rule for "the dice matched," not two different ones. Defeating a significant enemy may also bank a bonus Scene Die on top of that.

## Spending It

At your GM's discretion, for pacing and complications - a reinforcement arrives, a door jams, the weather turns, an NPC makes an unexpected choice. Think of it as the GM's version of spending a Pip: a visible, earned resource spent to shape what happens next.

**Compel-style bonus dice** are also part of the plan: your GM (or another player) can offer you a complication tied to your own Highlight or Flaw, and accepting it hands the GM a bonus Scene Die. The exact shape of that offer-and-accept exchange is still being worked out.
