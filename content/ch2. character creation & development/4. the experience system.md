# The Experience System

Project: Boundless replaces point-based XP with **Experiences** — a system that ties your growth directly to what your character actually does at the table.

## Structure

You may have up to **5 Experiences** at once. Every success you roll tags exactly one of them — never more, never zero — based on whichever best fits the fiction of that success.

Growth on a given Experience escalates in cost per tier: **3, then 5, then 7** notches for each successive tier. Hitting a growth milestone grants three things at once:

1. A **narrative privilege** — something fiction-facing you can now do or claim, not just a numeric bump.
2. A **free, once-per-scene step-up**.
3. **1 milestone tick** toward your next level.

*An Experience like "I never leave someone behind" might reach its first growth tier after 3 tagged successes. At that point you gain a narrative privilege built around it, a free step-up usable once per scene, and a tick toward your next level. The next tier costs 5 more tagged successes, then 7 after that.*

## Swapping Experiences

You can swap an Experience out for a different one at any time. Your accumulated notches carry over — swapping doesn't erase banked progress on an Experience you set aside. Using a *higher* tier of an Experience, though, is fiction-gated: you must narratively re-demonstrate the lower tiers before invoking a higher tier's privileges again. This stops a player from banking tier access on paper without ever earning it in play.

## Leveling Up

A full level-up happens once you've filled 5 milestone ticks — the current working number, not yet confirmed as final. Reaching a full level-up, not each individual tick, is what unlocks new progression-scale content, like further growth in your Identity (Ch2 §7).
