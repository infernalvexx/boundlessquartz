# The Experience System

Project: Boundless replaces point-based XP with **Experiences**: A system that ties your ambitions, your background and your goals directly to what your character does at the table.
## Structure
| Experience Type           |                                                                                                                                                                          |
| ------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Developing Experience** | The chosen Experience you wish to actively improve within a Scene                                                                                                        |
| **Active Experience**     | 4 out of your 5 Active Experiences give any benefits that their development provides, though they cannot be developed further unless designated a Developing Experience. |
| **Inactive Experience**   | Any Experience your character has under their belt that you may choose to swap in as an Active Experience between Scenes.                                                |
When creating a character you choose 3 Experiences that shape the basis of your character. As you grow you may have up to **5 Experiences** active at once, though this does not mean you may only have a total of 5 experiences. As you adventure through the world you might learn more about yourself, adding experiences to your repertoire.

Each Experience has [5 (tbd)] points of development to progress through. Each point gives a substantial boon when acquired. This boon may be a stat increase, stepping up a die or increasing your pip pool, it may be a specific ability tied to the intent of the Experience like a special Ability or it may be an enhancement to another of your characters traits, for example when Approaching an Experience's pool roll with a certain Approach, gain an increase to the result of that roll. 

1. A **narrative privilege** — something fiction-facing you can now do or claim, not just a numeric bump.
2. A **free, once-per-scene step-up**.
3. **1 milestone tick** toward your next level.

## Swapping Experiences
Between scenes you may choose to swap out active experiences with inactive experiences. This allows you to grow your character in a manner that suits your desires while also being able to utilise their unique experiences scene to scene. As each Experience has its own progression track, swapping out a Developing Experience to an Active Experience keeps the bonuses, though if an Experience that gives bonuses if moved to an Inactive Experience then it will stop providing its bonuses.

*You may hold both the Sword Wielder Experience that gives you benefits against fleshy targets, and the Fist Fighter Experiences that lends you bonuses against armored targets. Going into a new scene you know that the majority of your enemies will be armored meaning your Sword Wielder Experience will be less effective. At this stage you can swap the less effective Experience out for something that can help you in an area you might otherwise be lacking against this upcoming scene.
For example a Patient Fighter Experience that gives you defensive benefits when fighting could better suit the scene.*

## Developing Experiences
As you explore the world you will individually or as a group make narrative decisions that will drive the progression of your Experiences. As you move through a Scene the GM decides when you've earned a point of experience based on your actions.
Alternatively, drive to accomplish universal goals that will develop your Experiences for all involved, for example: 
Defeating a Major Enemy within a Scene, assisting to Bond with an Elemental Sprite, Finding a Secret within a Scene, uncovering a plot within the World over multiple Scenes, or whatever else your GM deems worthy of character development.
