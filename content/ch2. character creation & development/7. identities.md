# Identities

Once you reach **Kindled tier (Level 3)**, it's time to give your character a signature. Before that point, you're building your Legacy's first abilities out of the shared baseline Building Blocks (Ch3 §1) and getting comfortable with your character sheet — Identities are deliberately gated behind Level 3 so they don't overwhelm you before you've found your footing.

## Choosing a Signature

Look through the list below and decide on the signature expression and style of character you want to grow into. This list is only a taste of what's possible in Boundless — pick one outright, or bring an idea to your GM and work out something bespoke together. If a homebrewed signature doesn't end up feeling fun in play, you're always free to try something else, or fall back on one of the list below.

| Identity | Unique Resource/Mechanic | Identity Goal |
|---|---|---|
| Feed your blade with magic to devastating effect | **Charge Counters** — bank charge *on the weapon itself* to unleash whenever you please | Charge the blade through casting, cash it in through melee. |
| Use your blood to defend your party | **Spend Blood Pips Directly** — Resource Protection is inverted | Be the one taking the hits. |
| Not getting hit is the key to success | **Focus** — build Focus through being targeted by your enemies | Delay melee for as long as possible, punish those who chase you. |
| More Exhaustion? More Power! | **Turn Blood Pips into Verve Pips** as you please | Trade blows to come out on top. |
| Cultivate a bond with your element, raise a companion | **Elemental Construct** — gain a tactical advantage through bonus resources | Field and puppet a semi-independent second combatant. |
| Keep away from enemies until you're ready to engage | **Patience** — each action not spent harming an opponent builds Patience | Unleash your Patient Hammer to devastating effect, or stand strong in your Patient Shield. |
| Awareness of your surroundings keeps everyone alive | **Transfuse** — hitting clean steals a pip which you can give to an ally | Target high-value enemies, restore in-need allies. |
| Getting hurt is the plan | Less Blood Pips gives positive yet detrimental bonuses | Challenge death to unleash the pinnacle of your strength. |
| Intrinsically link yourself to another | **Bond** — share resources with your chosen partner | Provide utility and support in an unparalleled manner. |

## Why This Comes After Building Blocks, Not Before

Identity is a specialization layer on top of your Legacy, not a replacement for it. Your Building Blocks and your Skills/Approaches already define what your character can fundamentally do; Identity gives that foundation a signature hook — a resource, a rhythm, a reason your kit feels like *yours* and not just a generic list of abilities.
