# Recovery Die

The Recovery Die is your table's shared resource for heroic moments.

## How It Works

You get **1 Recovery Die per player present** in the scene, pooled and shared at the table — not a personal, individually-owned resource. Spend one on a genuine heroic moment tied to your character's Highlight or Flaw.

## Sizing

The die's size is set by the scene's tension, across five tiers:

**None → d4 → d6 → d8 → d10**

A **half-pip top-up** is available only at the two hardest tension tiers (d8 and d10) — the highest-stakes scenes get a little extra generosity without diluting the value of lower-tension scenes.

## Persistence

Recovery Dice don't carry over between scenes. Table pressure resets fresh each scene — you can't hoard them.

## Compel-Style Bonus Dice

Your GM, or another player, can offer you a complication tied to your own Highlight or Flaw. Accepting it grants a bonus Recovery Die to the table's shared pool. The exact mechanism for this exchange is still being worked out.
