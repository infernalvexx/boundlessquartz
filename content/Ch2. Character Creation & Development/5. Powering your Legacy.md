# Powering Your Legacy

Once you've undergone your First Bonding ([Ch2 p8](obsidian://open?vault=TTRPG&file=Master%2F1.%20Version%202%2FCh2.%20Character%20Creation%20%26%20Development%2F8.%20Sprite%20Bonding)) and formed a working tie to an element, that bond needs ongoing feeding. **Sustaining Practices** as seen below are the list of methods a practitioner continues to both draw their element in and empower themselves after the fact.

Pick one, or several, that fit your character's personality and circumstances: these work as roleplaying hooks as much as mechanical ones. None of them are tied to any specific culture and is meant to help you define your own Legacy; treat this list as a menu to choose from, not a fixed assignment. Secrets throughout the world may hide unique (GM created and/or player collaboration developed) practices for you to find.

1. Meditation: Slow, calculated breathing, drawing ambient elemental presence over time. The baseline every other practice varies from.
2. 3. **Resource Consumption**: consuming an elemental-imbued item directly. Pull the power from items, destroying them in the process, to improve the quality or quantity of that Element within your control.
3. **Sparring Communion**: ritualised combat with a trusted partner; power drawn from controlled conflict.
4. **Vigil Fasting**: Abstaining from food or comfort near a Boundless or Prime site to sustain yourself with the magical bounty of the world instead of mortal bonds.
5. **Blood Tithe**: Willingly spend Blood as an offering to an object, Elemental or patron, converted into growth over time.
6. **Sprite Communion**: sustained, focused time near a specific Sprites, building an informal relationship and potential link.
7. **Dream Walking**: power drawn during sleep, especially useful near Dream-adjacent sites or elements.
8. **Scholarly Discourse**: Debating and teaching elemental theory, refining understanding through argument and study.
9. **Craftbinding**: Channel power through the act of specifically non elemental creation. Abilities and elements may be used to imbue items to enhance this effect.
10. **Nexus-Chasing**: seeking a Natural element's most extreme manifestation and surviving proximity to it.
11. **Silent Fast**: Abstaining from using your element entirely, letting pressure build before a deliberate release.
12. **Territorial Claiming**: Mark and tend land tied to your element, power drawn from sustained stewardship.
13. **Progeny Blessing**: An established practitioner formally passing power to a chosen successor or student. Ties directly into Lineage succession.
14. **Wild Hunt**: Tracking and besting a wild creature strongly associated with your element.
15. **Convergence Seeking**: Travelling to where two elements' influence overlaps or conflicts, drawing power from the friction.
