# Player Scene Die

When a scene is set, difficulty is presented and narrative is explained the GM decides how many and of what quality die to place in the middle of the table. These are Scene die, one for each player in the party.
# When Players earn Scene Die
Players rarely receive Scene Die outside of a new scene being introduced to the session. Even then it's possible that a very difficult scene will not refresh Scene Die. If you have a forgiving or generous GM they may choose to gift you scene die for unique, brave, dangerous or impactful actions - before OR after the pool is rolled.
## Spending Player Scene Die
At any point during the scene when you roll a pool of die you may use 1 scene die at your discretion. You may only choose to add a scene die to a roll BEFORE you roll. Whatever the result, drop the lowest die.
Your fellow party members may also do this at any point they make a roll once at any point. Alternatively you may discuss with your party a 'spotlight' method of play. If you've already used 1 scene die, ask your party if you can spotlight for this scene. 
### Spotlighting
Any other player that agrees to your request to spotlight gives you permission to use their scene die for this scene. When this occurs you can choose to use all available scene die to you in the same roll, keeping the highest 3 values.

# GM Scene Die

The GM Scene Die is a pacing tool your GM banks over the course of a scene and spends at their own discretion to introduce a complication, a twist, or a moment of real pressure that keeps the scene moving.
## When the GM Earns Scene Die
A Scene Die is banked whenever a roll comes up with **2 or 3 matching dice** results. Defeating a significant enemy may also bank a bonus Scene Die for the GM on top of that.
## Spending GM Scene Die

At your GM's discretion; 
>when reinforcement arrives, when a door jams, the weather turns, an NPC makes an unexpected choice.

Think of this as the GM's version of spending a GM Scene Die. This is a resource for the GM to either add a die to their own pool rolls OR to kick off unique events.
