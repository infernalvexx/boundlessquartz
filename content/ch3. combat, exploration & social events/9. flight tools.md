# Flight Tools

Flight Bands (Ch3 §8) are tied to Power Scale tier — but that doesn't mean flight is entirely locked away from lower-tier characters.

## Buying Access Early

Any Power Tier can buy flight-granting gear, regardless of whether their tier would naturally grant that Flight Band. Using flight-gear below the tier that would naturally grant it, though, drains Pips faster than the flight is actually worth — a self-enforcing soft tax, not a hard ban. You can fly early; it will cost you more than it's worth most of the time.

## The One Exception

Sprite Bonding (Ch2 §8) removes or reduces this tax. A strong enough tie to your bonded element makes flying via gear meaningfully cheaper than it would be for an unbonded character at the same tier — one of the confirmed hooks tying Sprite Bonding directly into the resource system, not just narrative flavor.
