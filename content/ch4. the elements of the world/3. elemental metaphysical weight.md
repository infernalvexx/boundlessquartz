# Elemental Metaphysical Weight

At **Ascendant tier (Level 7–8)** and intensifying through **Sovereign (Level 9–10)**, a practitioner begins passively radiating their bonded element in an expanding band around themselves. This is the mechanism by which a person, not just a place, becomes a living center of elemental gravity.

## The Core Loop

- The band **muffles rather than removes** other elements within it — non-native elements simply work noticeably worse nearby, not not-at-all.
- Naturally-occurring resources and treasures forming within the band lean toward the resident element, and can be consumed by other characters (**Treasure Consumption**, Ch2 §5) to grow their own power.
- This is self-reinforcing: a powerful Legacy or Lineage's territory keeps producing more of exactly what made it powerful in the first place.

**Why this is more than flavor:** it's the same proximity-enhancement logic the Elemental Suite already applies to Sprites, Boundless Elementals, and Primes (a campfire burns hotter near a Fire Sprite, more chaotic near a Fire Prime) — extended, for the first time, to *people* rather than *places*. A Sovereign-tier practitioner starts to behave like a living Boundless site.

## Mechanical Shape

- The band's radius scales **exponentially per individual Level**, not just per tier — a Level 10 Sovereign's band is dramatically larger than a Level 9's. The exact multiplier is still being tuned through play; the exponential *shape* of the curve is locked.
- Non-native elemental Uses inside the band take a step-down; the resident element's own Uses get a step-up.
- Treasures generated within the band automatically carry a bonus Use tied to the resident element.

This is deliberately **not** something your GM tracks with hard numbers or curves. Power concentration is followed qualitatively — narrated through where Ascendant and Sovereign-tier elementalists actually are and have been, rather than managed on a spreadsheet.

## Death and Succession

A **Sovereign-tier death births a genuine sacred site** — a major, lasting impact on the Reality plane, with a lesser echo on the Elemental Plane. This is the concrete mechanism by which new Boundless Elemental sites enter world history.

**Absent death**, a band wanes slowly once its source moves on — unless a comparable-tier elementalist moves in, in which case the band persists and re-centers on them. Presence alone is enough to hold or transfer a region; death isn't required for succession.

**Cross-element succession:** if a new elementalist of similar power but a *different* element moves into a region still carrying a prior resident's weight, the two **blend for a transitional period**, then the newcomer's element **fully replaces** the old one over a long span. This is the mechanism behind layered regional elemental history — a region known today for Water may still carry old Fire-flavored ruins from a Fire legend a generation prior.

## Open Questions Worth Knowing About

1. Is the band always involuntary and passive, or can a practitioner consciously suppress it?
2. Does **Progeny Blessing** (Sustaining Practice #21, Ch2 §5) let an heir inherit or extend a predecessor's existing band, or does every practitioner always start from zero regardless of lineage?
3. What happens where two *different* elements' bands overlap **without** a succession event — both practitioners alive and active at once? The blend-then-replace rule above only covers succession, not simultaneous live overlap.
4. How this site-creation mechanism relates to the Birth mechanic (Ch4 §2) — is a Weight-born sacred site *the same kind of event* as a Birth, or a separate system that happens to look similar?
