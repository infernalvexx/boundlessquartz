# Resource Pools

Everything you do at the table draws from three Core Resource Pools.

| Pool      | What it governs                            |
| --------- | ------------------------------------------ |
| **Blood** | Health, survival, injury resistance        |
| **Might** | Physical strength and willpower            |
| **Verve** | Attunement, sensitivity, arcane capability |

## Two Layers, Per Pool
Each Pool carries two separate layers:
1. **Your Three Die pool**: This pool grows only through levelling and the choices you make building your Legacy; you don't spend it moment to moment.
2. **Pool Pips**: A unique, spendable currency paired with Blood, Might & Verve, with its own independent refresh cycle. Pips are what you need to live and to act. Burn them to step a die, fuel an ability or defend them to preserve your life.

## Pool Pip Bonus
Your Pip capacity in each Pool grows on its own separate track with your character. Both the shared Level-Up Baseline and your Practitioner Type's own table ([Ch2. p2](obsidian://open?vault=TTRPG&file=Master%2F1.%20Version%202%2FCh2.%20Character%20Creation%20%26%20Development%2F2.%20Practitioners%20-%20Cast%2C%20Wield%2C%20Shape)) contribute a **Pool Pip Bonus** at each level, and the two stack together. 
A Caster's own table front-loads this early, a Wielder's later, each type pacing it differently. Add both numbers to your running Pip Cap on top of whatever your die-tier growth already provides.

## Resource Protection
By default, **Blood is a protected source of Pips**: before Blood damage lands, you can choose to spend Might or Verve Pips, one for one, to prevent Blood pip loss. Abilities can modify this ratio.
Some Legacies flip which resource is protected, spending Blood freely and directly instead, in exchange for a different trade-off elsewhere in that character's kit. If your Legacy or Identity inverts Resource Protection, note it clearly on your sheet, since it changes how everyone at the table should expect your Pools to behave.

*Petra Vane inverts the default outright — her Identity spends Blood pips directly to shield her allies instead of the other way around. Where a default-Protection character burns Might or Verve to keep Blood damage off, Petra burns Blood on purpose: 1 pip downgrades an incoming Hit against an adjacent ally to Blocked, and 2 redirects the hit onto her entirely. Her sheet flags the inversion clearly, because anyone assuming she plays by the default rule would misjudge how fast she's actually willing to bleed.*

Because Blood is protected by default, running low on Might or Verve tends to read as "spent, drained or out of tricks motif" while losing Blood reads as a hard read for danger and your distance from death.

## Die-Stepping
Your dice grow in two different ways:
**Permanent die-stepping.** 
When Levelling up your baseline of dice within your dice pool grows ([Ch2. p2](obsidian://open?vault=TTRPG&file=Master%2F1.%20Version%202%2FCh2.%20Character%20Creation%20%26%20Development%2F2.%20Practitioners%20-%20Cast%2C%20Wield%2C%20Shape)). Each Practitioner type further alters the growth of your dice pools. Outside of level up increases you may also find items that step your die up or down based on certain activities, whether it be combat, social or exploration activities.

**Temporary die-stepping.**
During a given scene there may be opportunity for temporarily die-stepping. Outside of these opportunities you may also spend pips to increase die-steps within your respective pools for one roll. This one-time die step will have varied cost based on the current level of the die you step up or down:
d4-d6 = 1 pip, d6-d8 = 1 pip & d8-d10 costs 2 pips.
