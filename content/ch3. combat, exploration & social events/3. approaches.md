# Approaches

If Skills answer "what are you doing," **Approaches** answer "how are you doing it." Approach is the wrapper term for eight flavor descriptors, arranged as four opposing pairs:

| Pair | Approach A | Approach B |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Flourish | Wit |
| 2 | Calculation | Passion |
| 3 | Charm | Resolve |
| 4 | Guile | Nerve |

## Playing an Approach

The four opposing pairs give you built-in characterization: leaning Flourish over Wit, or Passion over Calculation, says something true about *how* your character solves problems — even when two characters use the identical Skill for the identical goal.

## Approach and Speed

Every Approach carries its own value on a 0-to-+6 scale. Whichever Approach you choose for an action adds that value onto your Speed for next round (Ch3 §4), alongside a value carried by the action's Ability Type (Ch3 §1) — the two add together.

| Pair | Approach | Speed Value | Approach | Speed Value |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Flourish | 2 | Wit | 4 |
| 2 | Calculation | 1 | Passion | 6 |
| 3 | Charm | 2 | Resolve | 5 |
| 4 | Guile | 2 | Nerve | 4 |

Higher values are better: they push you earlier into next round's turn order. Choosing Passion over Calculation for the same action costs real tempo, even when the fictional outcome is identical either way — see Ch3 §4 for the full combined formula.

*(These 8 values are a direct +3 shift of an earlier draft's −3-to-+3 range onto the current 0-to-+6 scale, preserving the original relative spacing between Approaches.)*
