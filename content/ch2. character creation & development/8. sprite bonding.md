# Sprite Bonding

Sprite Bonding is your character's origin story with magic itself — the moment, or slow process, by which a practitioner first ties themselves to an element. How much cultural institution stands behind that moment says a lot about who your character is and where they come from.

## Major Methods

*(Institutionalized — these produce the bulk of the world's Lineages.)*

1. **Planar Trial of the Elements** — a formal, dangerous proving ground; *how* a candidate survives, not just whether they do, determines which element answers. Fits institutional, hierarchical cultures — the soft-canon origin for meritocratic, trial-tested practitioners.
2. **Scarring Rite** — power is only treated as real if it costs something visible. A deliberate wound or trial-by-combat forces an early, unstable bond, biased toward Blood/Sacrifice and other pain-adjacent elements.
3. **Scholar's Codex** — magic as applied science; formal academic study until resonance is understood intellectually, then confirmed physically. Slow, but produces well-rounded practitioners with a Sense-leaning bias.
4. **Choir of Names** — a communal rite: an entire cohort bonds to one shared element together, with individual specialization branching off afterward. The clearest case of a Lineage coming *before* an individual Legacy. Fits Council-of-Elders or Hive-Collective style cultures.
5. **Still Water** — pure environmental osmosis near a Prime or Boundless site. The bond is absorbed unconsciously over childhood, usually only noticed retroactively when a first ability manifests during real emotional extremity.

## Solitary Methods

*(For wanderers and nomads — recognized, but hands-off.)*

6. **Drifting Accord** — no fixed bonding. Practitioners carry portable totems and negotiate temporary, renewable accords with Sprites encountered while migrating. A Legacy built this way shifts in flavor as its people move.
7. **Wandering Vigil** — a solitary rite: sent alone into unclaimed wilderness for a fixed span, and whichever Sprite finds you becomes your first tie. Works equally well as a sanctioned cultural practice, or as something a lone straggler does with no cultural backing at all.

## After First Bonding

Once bonded, an element doesn't sustain itself for free — see **Powering Your Legacy** (Ch2 §5) for the ongoing practices that keep a bond fed. **Cradle Cycling**, once treated as its own origin method, is now categorized as Sustaining Practice #1 — the baseline ongoing practice every other one varies from, not a way to first bond at all.

## A Note on Absence

Not every bond is to a presence. World lore holds that a dedicated Sprite exists specifically to represent the *absence* of element — worth keeping in mind if your character's story is about what magic **isn't** there, rather than what is.
