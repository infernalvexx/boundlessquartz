# Birth and Death of the Elements

## Birth
Any element, **Natural or Bound**, can be freshly born into the world. Several questions about Birth remain open, even to those who study it closely:
- Whether Birth answers to any trigger condition at all, or arises purely from circumstance.
- Whether a newly-Born element always enters at Sprite stage ([Ch4. p1](obsidian://open?vault=TTRPG&file=Master%2F1.%20Version%202%2FCh4.%20The%20Elements%20of%20the%20World%2F1.%20The%20Elementals%20of%20Boundless)), or can appear anywhere on the scale of strength.
- Whether Planar elements, tied specifically to inter-planar interaction, can be Born the same way Natural and Bound elements can, or whether their very nature excludes them from the process.

## Death
Elements themselves don't "die" in a literal sense the way a creature does, but a region's *relationship* to an element may very well end, most concretely through **Metaphysical Weight succession** ([Ch4. p3](obsidian://open?vault=TTRPG&file=Master%2F1.%20Version%202%2FCh4.%20The%20Elements%20of%20the%20World%2F3.%20Elemental%20Metaphysical%20Weight)): when a powerful practitioner dies, or is replaced by one bonded to a different element, the old elemental character of a region can fade, blend, or be fully overwritten either immediately or over time.
