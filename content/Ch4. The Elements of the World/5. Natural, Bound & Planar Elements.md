# Natural, Bound & Planar Elements

Every element in the world sorts into one of three categories:

- **Natural** — physical/elemental phenomena. The largest category: 21 base elements plus 28 combinations built from them, 49 total.
- **Bound** — tied to human concept, craft, and convention rather than raw physical phenomena. 16 elements.
- **Planar** — specifically tied to inter-planar interaction and Sprite Bonding. 6 elements.

*(An older fourth category, "Other," has been fully retired — everything that would once have landed there is now reclassified into one of the three above.)*

**Two naming collisions are intentional, not errors:**
- The element **Sovereign** (Planar) shares its name with Power Scale Tier 5 (Ch2 §6) — deliberate double duty, the same way the tier name itself does double duty as political influence (Ch5 §5).
- The element **Planar** (Natural) shares its name with the Planar *category* itself.

Every element below is presented with the same four fields: how it **manifests** on a practitioner, what it's commonly **used** for, the emotional **vibes** it carries, and how it behaves differently on the **Planar** plane versus in **Reality**.

## Natural — Base (21)

The unmixed elemental roots. Every combination element below is built from two of these (with one exception: Crystal draws on Earth + Arcane).

### Arcane
- **Manifestation:** Faint colorless shimmer or held breath of stillness around the user, with arcs of sluggish purple/blue electricity.
- **Uses:** Veil intent from spiritual senses; calm a panicking crowd; cleanse lingering corruption.
- **Vibes:** Serenity, unease, detachment.
- **Planar Behavior:** Pools in undisturbed hollows, arcs between different coalescing elements.
- **Reality Behavior:** Thins nearby ambient magic, muffles sound and sensation.

### Light
- **Manifestation:** Directed beams, glare, a soft glow at the user's skin and eyes.
- **Uses:** Blind an enemy; signal across distance; reveal hidden ink or wards.
- **Vibes:** Hope, exposure, urgency.
- **Planar Behavior:** Streaks fast and directionally, rarely lingers.
- **Reality Behavior:** Travels in straight lines, casts hard shadows.

### Shadow
- **Manifestation:** Deepened darkness, a silhouette that thickens or detaches.
- **Uses:** Conceal movement; muffle sound; smother a small flame.
- **Vibes:** Secrecy, dread, comfort.
- **Planar Behavior:** Pools in low, still places, spreads slowly.
- **Reality Behavior:** Clings to corners, recedes from direct light.

### Force
- **Manifestation:** A visible pressure-ripple, air distorting like heat haze.
- **Uses:** Shove an enemy back; brace a collapsing structure; redirect a projectile.
- **Vibes:** Assertion, impact, control.
- **Planar Behavior:** Radiates outward from a point then dissipates.
- **Reality Behavior:** Transfers instantly on contact, obeys momentum.

### Life
- **Manifestation:** Warm green-gold glow, quickened pulse, faster healing.
- **Uses:** Mend a wound; accelerate plant growth; steady a dying heartbeat.
- **Vibes:** Warmth, urgency, renewal.
- **Planar Behavior:** Threads through everything living, never fully absent.
- **Reality Behavior:** Concentrates in blood, sap, breath.

### Death
- **Manifestation:** Cold gray pallor, wilting plants, a chill in the air.
- **Uses:** Hasten decay of an obstacle; read how close someone is to dying; sap vigor from a foe.
- **Vibes:** Finality, sorrow, peace.
- **Planar Behavior:** Settles like sediment, gathers at old battle sites.
- **Reality Behavior:** Accelerates decomposition, dulls color and sound.

### Destruction
- **Manifestation:** Fine cracks spreading across a surface before it crumbles.
- **Uses:** Shatter a lock or wall; unmake a lesser construct; disintegrate refuse.
- **Vibes:** Catharsis, rage, loss.
- **Planar Behavior:** Erodes the edges of other currents it touches.
- **Reality Behavior:** Propagates through structural weak points.

### Lightning
- **Manifestation:** White-neon blue crackling arcs across the user's skin or weapon.
- **Uses:** Stun a target; power a device; split wood or stone along a seam.
- **Vibes:** Shock, exhilaration, danger.
- **Planar Behavior:** Leaps erratically between charged points.
- **Reality Behavior:** Seeks the shortest path to ground.

### Fire
- **Manifestation:** Literal flame wreathing hands, weapon, or eyes.
- **Uses:** Burn or cauterize; light the way; forge metal.
- **Vibes:** Passion, hunger, destruction.
- **Planar Behavior:** Burns brighter and needs no fuel.
- **Reality Behavior:** Consumes fuel, spreads by contact.

### Earth
- **Manifestation:** Stone-textured skin, a grounded stance, faint tremors underfoot.
- **Uses:** Raise a wall; anchor against a push; sense vibrations through ground.
- **Vibes:** Stability, patience, stubbornness.
- **Planar Behavior:** Shifts slowly, remembers old shapes.
- **Reality Behavior:** Obeys gravity, resists sudden change.

### Water
- **Manifestation:** A damp sheen, fluid movement, pooling at the user's feet.
- **Uses:** Douse fire; carve stone slowly; carry a message downstream.
- **Vibes:** Adaptability, calm, grief.
- **Planar Behavior:** Flows toward other water currents, merges freely.
- **Reality Behavior:** Seeks the lowest point, takes the shape of its container.

### Wind
- **Manifestation:** A constant light breeze following the user.
- **Uses:** Carry a scent or sound; clear smoke; unbalance a foe's footing.
- **Vibes:** Freedom, restlessness, clarity.
- **Planar Behavior:** Never fully still, drifts between all other currents.
- **Reality Behavior:** Obeys pressure differentials, carries scent and sound.

### Poison
- **Manifestation:** Faint discoloration in veins or a bitter smell.
- **Uses:** Weaken a foe over time; preserve meat by curing; test food for safety.
- **Vibes:** Paranoia, patience, malice.
- **Planar Behavior:** Spreads slowly through contact with other currents.
- **Reality Behavior:** Requires ingestion, injection, or contact to act.

### Metal
- **Manifestation:** Faint metallic sheen on skin or a magnetic hum.
- **Uses:** Reinforce a weapon's edge; locate or erupt buried ore; conduct a charge.
- **Vibes:** Resolve, coldness, permanence.
- **Planar Behavior:** Sits inert, resists being moved.
- **Reality Behavior:** Conducts, resists deformation, sparks against Static.

### Sound
- **Manifestation:** A faint resonant hum audible only near the user.
- **Uses:** Carry a message silently; shatter glass with a precise pitch; deafen an enemy briefly.
- **Vibes:** Connection, alarm, focus.
- **Planar Behavior:** Travels instantly between distant points.
- **Reality Behavior:** Requires a medium, fades with distance.

### Hollow
- **Manifestation:** A faint absence/numbness — sound, warmth, and color read slightly muted nearby.
- **Uses:** Dampen or nullify another element's effect; create true silence; mark a place as empty, disconnecting it from elemental effect.
- **Vibes:** Dread, peace, emptiness.
- **Planar Behavior:** The "floor" all other currents rest on.
- **Reality Behavior:** Never directly perceptible, only inferred by its absence.

### Omen
- **Manifestation:** An involuntary shiver or gut-certainty preceding significant events.
- **Uses:** Sense something significant is about to happen (not what); warn allies of unspecified danger; read a place's history of misfortune.
- **Vibes:** Dread, anticipation, helplessness.
- **Planar Behavior:** Felt strongly, understood clearly.
- **Reality Behavior:** Felt vaguely, almost always misread until after the fact.

### Dream
- **Manifestation:** Eyes going distant/unfocused, faint whispered fragments of speech.
- **Uses:** Implant a suggestion while someone sleeps; glimpse a fragment of another's fear or desire; escape briefly into unconsciousness at will.
- **Vibes:** Wonder, dread, longing.
- **Planar Behavior:** Close to its native home, vivid and stable.
- **Reality Behavior:** Fades quickly upon waking, hard to sustain.

### Blood
- **Manifestation:** Veins visibly darkening or pulsing beneath the user's skin.
- **Uses:** Fuel a technique with one's own vitality; track a target by a shared blood-tie; use Blood as an all-purpose temporary physically manifested tool.
- **Vibes:** Intimacy, sacrifice, hunger.
- **Planar Behavior:** Pools, thickens, and bubbles unnaturally.
- **Reality Behavior:** Requires an actual wound or sample to work with.

### Bone
- **Manifestation:** Joints creaking audibly, a pale cast to the skin.
- **Uses:** Reinforce a structure with calcified growth; command remains that share a connection; read a death from its skeleton alone.
- **Vibes:** Reverence, unease, permanence.
- **Planar Behavior:** Assembles and reassembles freely.
- **Reality Behavior:** Requires actual bone or calcified material present.

### Planar
- **Manifestation:** The user's edges feel faintly less fixed to reality than normal, blurring in vision like a heat haze.
- **Uses:** Thin the barrier between planes for oneself or another; sense when something nearby has recently crossed between planes; sense a Prime's proximity before its effects become obvious.
- **Vibes:** Vertigo, freedom, dread.
- **Planar Behavior:** This is simply how things naturally move here.
- **Reality Behavior:** Crossing planes is difficult and always noticed.

## Natural — Combinations (28)

Each entry lists its two parent currents.

### Magma *(Fire + Earth)*
- **Manifestation:** Glowing veins cracking across stone-toned skin.
- **Uses:** Melt a barrier; forge in the field; seal a wound with heat.
- **Vibes:** Overwhelm, slow fury, transformation.
- **Planar Behavior:** Churns beneath other currents, rarely surfaces.
- **Reality Behavior:** Cools and hardens on contact with air.

### Cloud *(Water + Wind)*
- **Manifestation:** A personal haze or mist trailing the user.
- **Uses:** Obscure vision; carry water long distances; soften a fall.
- **Vibes:** Mystery, detachment, wistfulness.
- **Planar Behavior:** Drifts freely, rarely holds a fixed shape.
- **Reality Behavior:** Condenses with cold, disperses with wind.

### Gravity *(Force + Earth)*
- **Manifestation:** A faint downward distortion, objects nearby feel heavier.
- **Uses:** Pin an enemy in place; stabilize a falling structure; increase impact force.
- **Vibes:** Dread, inevitability, weight.
- **Planar Behavior:** Warps other currents passing near it.
- **Reality Behavior:** Always points toward mass, never fully absent.

### Static *(Lightning + Metal)*
- **Manifestation:** Hair standing on end, small sparks at fingertips.
- **Uses:** Deliver a controlled shock; disable a mechanism; charge a device.
- **Vibes:** Anticipation, irritation, focus.
- **Planar Behavior:** Builds slowly then discharges all at once.
- **Reality Behavior:** Seeks conductive material.

### Steam *(Fire + Water)*
- **Manifestation:** A constant faint mist rising from the user's skin.
- **Uses:** Obscure and scald simultaneously; sterilize a wound; power a mechanism.
- **Vibes:** Urgency, discomfort, cleansing.
- **Planar Behavior:** Rises and thins quickly.
- **Reality Behavior:** Scalds on contact, condenses on cool surfaces.

### Ice *(Water + Death)*
- **Manifestation:** Frost creeping across nearby surfaces, breath visible.
- **Uses:** Freeze a surface for footing; preserve a body or wound; blunt a blade's edge.
- **Vibes:** Stillness, isolation, clarity.
- **Planar Behavior:** Holds its shape longer than in reality.
- **Reality Behavior:** Melts with heat, cracks under pressure.

### Ash *(Fire + Destruction)*
- **Manifestation:** Fine gray dust trailing the user's movements.
- **Uses:** Mark a trail; smother embers; irritate an enemy's eyes.
- **Vibes:** Aftermath, mourning, quiet.
- **Planar Behavior:** Settles everywhere, never fully clears.
- **Reality Behavior:** Scatters on wind, smothers small flames.

### Wood *(Life + Earth)*
- **Manifestation:** A faint bark-like texture on skin, green tint to the eyes.
- **Uses:** Grow a barrier of roots; splint a broken bone; craft a weapon that mends itself slowly.
- **Vibes:** Patience, growth, resilience.
- **Planar Behavior:** Threads and branches toward light-adjacent currents.
- **Reality Behavior:** Bends before it breaks, burns readily.

### Dust *(Earth + Wind)*
- **Manifestation:** A faint grit in the air around the user.
- **Uses:** Obscure tracks; irritate or blind at range; erode a surface slowly over time.
- **Vibes:** Neglect, time, erosion.
- **Planar Behavior:** Drifts endlessly, infrequently settles.
- **Reality Behavior:** Settles on still surfaces, scatters on disturbance.

### Storm *(Lightning + Cloud)*
- **Manifestation:** A personal weather-front; muffled, distant thunder emitted from the user's footsteps.
- **Uses:** Call down a strike on a single point; drive back a fleet with wind and rain; drown out sound entirely.
- **Vibes:** Awe, terror, release.
- **Planar Behavior:** Gathers other weather-currents into itself.
- **Reality Behavior:** Builds pressure before releasing violently.

### Typhoon *(Storm + Water)*
- **Manifestation:** A rotating wall of wind and rain around the user.
- **Uses:** Level a settlement's outer defenses; flood a battlefield; carry a vessel vast distances.
- **Vibes:** Helplessness, awe, grief.
- **Planar Behavior:** Consumes lesser Water and Wind currents nearby.
- **Reality Behavior:** Strengthens over open water, weakens over land.

### Eclipse *(Light + Shadow)*
- **Manifestation:** A ring of false twilight surrounding the user regardless of hour.
- **Uses:** Neutralize both blinding and concealing tricks at once; disorient reliance on Light or Shadow; mark a moment as historically significant.
- **Vibes:** Reverence, unease, finality.
- **Planar Behavior:** Briefly silences all nearby elemental activity.
- **Reality Behavior:** Rare and always noticed, often treated as an omen.

### Polarity *(Metal + Static)*
- **Manifestation:** Nearby metal trembles faintly toward or away from the user.
- **Uses:** Disarm a metal weapon at range; navigate without landmarks; weld two metal surfaces.
- **Vibes:** Attraction, aversion, certainty.
- **Planar Behavior:** Pulls related currents toward or away from itself.
- **Reality Behavior:** Only affects specific materials.

### Rot *(Life + Death)*
- **Manifestation:** Nearby organic matter ages visibly faster.
- **Uses:** Break down a corpse or waste quickly; weaken a wooden structure; ripen fruit instantly.
- **Vibes:** Disgust, inevitability, mercy.
- **Planar Behavior:** Spreads wherever Life and Death currents overlap.
- **Reality Behavior:** Requires organic material to act on.

### Erosion *(Water + Earth)*
- **Manifestation:** Fine sediment trailing wherever the user walks.
- **Uses:** Wear away a barrier over time; carve a channel for water; smooth a rough surface.
- **Vibes:** Patience, inevitability, loss.
- **Planar Behavior:** Acts instantly rather than gradually.
- **Reality Behavior:** Requires repeated contact to show effect.

### Fog *(Water + Shadow)*
- **Manifestation:** A low mist clinging close to the ground around the user.
- **Uses:** Conceal a retreat; muffle footsteps; disorient pursuers.
- **Vibes:** Uncertainty, calm, dread.
- **Planar Behavior:** Settles and holds shape unusually long.
- **Reality Behavior:** Burns off with sun or wind.

### Tide *(Water + Gravity)*
- **Manifestation:** A slow, rhythmic pull felt by anyone nearby.
- **Uses:** Draw water toward a point over time; predict coastal danger; slowly reclaim lost ground from the sea.
- **Vibes:** Patience, cycle, longing.
- **Planar Behavior:** Pulses on a fixed, unhurried rhythm.
- **Reality Behavior:** Tied to celestial bodies, cannot be rushed.

### Quake *(Earth + Force)*
- **Manifestation:** Trembling to quaking airwaves radiate from the user.
- **Uses:** Collapse a structure's foundation; knock enemies off balance; reveal a hidden passage via fault line.
- **Vibes:** Panic, power, upheaval.
- **Planar Behavior:** Travels instantly through connected Earth currents.
- **Reality Behavior:** Weakens rapidly with distance from origin.

### Landslide *(Earth + Gravity)*
- **Manifestation:** Loose stone and soil trembling as if eager to fall.
- **Uses:** Bury a pursuing force; clear a blocked path violently; redirect a river's course.
- **Vibes:** Inevitability, destruction, release.
- **Planar Behavior:** Gathers momentum before crossing into reality.
- **Reality Behavior:** Needs an unstable slope to act on.

### Avalanche *(Ice + Gravity)*
- **Manifestation:** A faint groaning in nearby snow or ice.
- **Uses:** Bury pursuers under snow; clear a mountain pass violently; signal danger to those who listen.
- **Vibes:** Dread, majesty, helplessness.
- **Planar Behavior:** Builds silently before crossing into reality.
- **Reality Behavior:** Requires accumulated snow or ice to act on.

### Wildfire *(Fire + Wind)*
- **Manifestation:** Embers that refuse to fully extinguish near the user.
- **Uses:** Clear vast tracts of land quickly; create an uncontrollable diversion; signal with smoke.
- **Vibes:** Panic, awe, loss.
- **Planar Behavior:** Spreads instantly across connected Fire currents.
- **Reality Behavior:** Ignites primed debris, quickly spreads as it comes to blaze, dies out quickly.

### Quicksand *(Earth + Water)*
- **Manifestation:** Solid ground beneath the user's feet turning faintly unstable.
- **Uses:** Trap a pursuer; soften a hard fall; disguise a pitfall.
- **Vibes:** Panic, helplessness, cunning.
- **Planar Behavior:** Behaves like any other Earth current, no instability.
- **Reality Behavior:** Requires oversaturated ground to act.

### Marsh *(Water + Wood)*
- **Manifestation:** Damp, faintly rooted skin, a smell of standing water.
- **Uses:** Slow pursuers through difficult terrain; grow rapid concealment; purify stagnant water.
- **Vibes:** Patience, decay, hidden life.
- **Planar Behavior:** Teems with overlapping Life and Water currents.
- **Reality Behavior:** Sopping wet earth and tangled undergrowth spreads throughout shallow waters.

### Mirage *(Light + Dust)*
- **Manifestation:** The air shimmers faintly, doubling distant shapes.
- **Uses:** Disguise one's true position; exhaust pursuers on a false trail; hide a structure in plain sight.
- **Vibes:** Deception, exhaustion, wonder.
- **Planar Behavior:** Images shown are genuine glimpses of the Elemental Plane.
- **Reality Behavior:** Requires heat and open sky to sustain.

### Crystal *(Earth + Arcane)*
- **Manifestation:** Faceted growths along the user's skin or weapon.
- **Uses:** Focus and amplify another element's effect; create a lasting marker or ward; store a small charge.
- **Vibes:** Clarity, patience, fragility.
- **Planar Behavior:** Grows slowly, holds its shape almost perfectly.
- **Reality Behavior:** Shatters under sudden force.

### Blight *(Poison + Death)*
- **Manifestation:** Nearby crops or foliage visibly sicken.
- **Uses:** Destroy an enemy's food supply; hasten a diseased creature's death; mark cursed ground.
- **Vibes:** Despair, quiet menace, warning.
- **Planar Behavior:** Spreads only where Poison and Death already overlap.
- **Reality Behavior:** Causes vulnerable organic life, perpetuates in naturally fitting settings.

### Frost *(Water + Shadow)*
- **Manifestation:** Delicate crystalline patterns forming on nearby surfaces.
- **Uses:** Mark a surface without damaging it; signal a warning subtly; gradually slow mechanisms.
- **Vibes:** Delicacy, quiet warning, fleeting beauty.
- **Planar Behavior:** Forms and unforms instantly.
- **Reality Behavior:** Melts with the first warmth.

### Ember *(Fire + Ash)*
- **Manifestation:** A faint persistent warmth in the user's chest or hands.
- **Uses:** Carry a spark across a long journey; comfort without danger of open flame.
- **Vibes:** Hope, quiet resilience, nostalgia.
- **Planar Behavior:** Never fully extinguishes.
- **Reality Behavior:** Slowly spreads embers from an epicenter, dies out but forms waves of slow-burning kindling.

## Bound (16)

Elements tied to human concept, craft, and convention rather than raw physical phenomena.

### Sword
- **Manifestation:** An edge appearing sharper or more certain than physics allow.
- **Uses:** Cut something conceptually "uncuttable," like a tie or an oath; sharpen resolve as much as a blade; parry the unparryable.
- **Vibes:** Conviction, violence, clarity.
- **Planar Behavior:** Every edge here is somehow the "true" edge.
- **Reality Behavior:** Needs an actual bladed object to channel through.

### Song
- **Manifestation:** An audible hum only the user (or intended listener) can hear.
- **Uses:** Carry an emotion across a language barrier; call for aid across impossible distance; unravel a Silence-based ward.
- **Vibes:** Joy, grief, longing.
- **Planar Behavior:** Carries flawlessly across any distance.
- **Reality Behavior:** Fades and distorts with distance like any sound.

### Glyph
- **Manifestation:** Faint marks appearing briefly on the user's skin when concentrating.
- **Uses:** Bind an effect permanently into an object; ward a threshold against a specific named threat; record a message only the intended reader can perceive.
- **Vibes:** Precision, secrecy, permanence.
- **Planar Behavior:** Glyphs here are alive, shifting and self-correcting.
- **Reality Behavior:** Fixed and inert once written, charged with an element that burns until triggered or faded.

### Oath
- **Manifestation:** Faint ink-like lines appearing on any surface a formal agreement is struck upon, physically restrictive.
- **Uses:** Formalize/enforce a binding trade between parties; detect the fine print's true intent; nullify an agreement made under duress; detect broken contracts.
- **Vibes:** Bonds, suspicion, conviction.
- **Planar Behavior:** Unbroken contracts burn with visible brightness and power.
- **Reality Behavior:** Enforceable only if consented, and exploitable by loopholes like any mundane contract.

### Mimic
- **Manifestation:** A faint blurring of the user's features, or misleading reflections and features flickering like shadows.
- **Uses:** Assume a false identity convincingly; mirror observed abilities imperfectly; see through another mimic's disguise; show truths to the mimic's host.
- **Vibes:** Vanity, deceit, relief.
- **Planar Behavior:** Every identity here is equally "true."
- **Reality Behavior:** Mundane observation can still see through a poorly maintained mask.

### Chain
- **Manifestation:** Faint linked marks appearing across the user's wrists, or chains dangling from shoulders when binding something.
- **Uses:** Physically restrain a target beyond normal strength; bind two fates together, for better or worse; break a Chain someone else has laid.
- **Vibes:** Restriction, security, resentment.
- **Planar Behavior:** Chains here can bind concepts, not just bodies.
- **Reality Behavior:** Still bound by the physical strength of the material used.

### War
- **Manifestation:** A cold clarity settling over the user's judgment in conflict.
- **Uses:** Coordinate allies with unnatural precision; recognize an enemy's strategy before it unfolds; steel a force's resolve before a losing fight.
- **Vibes:** Focus, dread, unity.
- **Planar Behavior:** Gathers and organizes other Bound currents around it.
- **Reality Behavior:** Only manifests where organized, intentional conflict is already underway.

### Ink
- **Manifestation:** Faint dark trails following the user's fingertips.
- **Uses:** Write something that cannot be unwritten by mundane means; erase a Glyph someone else has laid; stain something as a permanent mark of shame or honor.
- **Vibes:** Permanence, secrecy, judgment.
- **Planar Behavior:** Ink here never fully dries or settles, writhes around like snakes.
- **Reality Behavior:** Ordinary ink, ordinary limitations.

### Silence
- **Manifestation:** A deliberate, heavy quiet the user can project outward.
- **Uses:** Enforce a heavy quiet over a space; withhold a truth so completely it becomes a kind of power; unravel a Song-based effect.
- **Vibes:** Tension, relief, dread.
- **Planar Behavior:** Silence here is total and absolute.
- **Reality Behavior:** Still vulnerable to sufficiently loud, sudden mundane noise.

### Echo
- **Manifestation:** The user's actions echo faintly, shortly behind their initial action.
- **Uses:** Amplify or muffle a subsequent action's effect; detect a hidden connection between two things; synchronize a group's efforts, or make opponents dissonant.
- **Vibes:** Harmony, unease, connection.
- **Planar Behavior:** Nearly everything resonates or persists indefinitely.
- **Reality Behavior:** Fades quickly, usually within moments.

### Dissolve
- **Manifestation:** Loose threads or flakes — literal or figurative — appearing on anything under strain nearby.
- **Uses:** Find and exploit the weak point in an otherwise sound structure or plan; hasten something's inevitable collapse; recognize when something is unraveling before it's obvious.
- **Vibes:** Dread, inevitability, release.
- **Planar Behavior:** Happens instantly, across both planes at once.
- **Reality Behavior:** Usually gradual and visible in advance.

### Taboo
- **Manifestation:** An involuntary revulsion approaching a forbidden act.
- **Uses:** Enforce a genuine deterrent against a specific forbidden act; recognize when someone has broken an unspoken rule; weaponize a target's own sense of forbiddance.
- **Vibes:** Revulsion, restraint, guilt.
- **Planar Behavior:** Taboos here carry real, tangible weight, manifesting as formless monsters.
- **Reality Behavior:** Only as strong as the culture that holds it.

### Ritual
- **Manifestation:** The user's repeated, deliberate actions gain a faint accumulating weight.
- **Uses:** Strengthen an effect through genuine repetition over time; recognize when someone else's ritual is nearing completion; disrupt a ritual by breaking its pattern.
- **Vibes:** Devotion, patience, dread.
- **Planar Behavior:** Rituals here complete instantly once truly begun.
- **Reality Behavior:** Requires the full, uninterrupted repetition to succeed.

### Curse
- **Manifestation:** A faint discoloration or ill luck trailing whoever it's placed on.
- **Uses:** Bind a lasting ill fortune to a specific wrongdoer; recognize a Curse's true origin and intended trigger; lift a Curse by fulfilling its hidden condition.
- **Vibes:** Dread, justice, malice.
- **Planar Behavior:** Curses here are visible threads, easy to trace but highly infectious.
- **Reality Behavior:** Often invisible until its effect triggers.

### Sacrifice
- **Manifestation:** A visible cost the user pays plainly, with no way to hide it.
- **Uses:** Trade something precious for a proportionally powerful effect; recognize when someone else's offer is a Sacrifice in disguise; honor a past Sacrifice to strengthen the present.
- **Vibes:** Loss, conviction, reverence.
- **Planar Behavior:** The trade is felt instantly and completely here, birthing a pillar of the sacrificed material that bleeds loss and commitment.
- **Reality Behavior:** The cost may be delayed, deferred, or paid gradually.

### Time
- **Manifestation:** The user's perceived pace of events subtly diverging from everyone else's.
- **Uses:** Perceive a fraction of a second further ahead than normal; stretch a single moment's felt duration; recognize when something is genuinely out of its proper time.
- **Vibes:** Urgency, vertigo, dread.
- **Planar Behavior:** Flows inconsistently, sometimes doubling back.
- **Reality Behavior:** Stubbornly linear, resists almost all manipulation.

## Planar (6)

Elements tied specifically to inter-planar interaction and Sprite Bonding.

### Legacy
- **Manifestation:** A faint sense of ancestors watching, felt rather than seen.
- **Uses:** Draw on a bloodline's accumulated skill or knowledge; honor the dead in a way that strengthens the living; recognize an impostor's false lineage.
- **Vibes:** Pride, burden, continuity.
- **Planar Behavior:** The past and present overlap visibly here.
- **Reality Behavior:** Only as strong as the records and memory that support it, manifests as a superimposed image.

### Sovereign
- **Manifestation:** Involuntary deference from those nearby, a weight to the user's voice.
- **Uses:** Compel obedience within a controlled domain; silence a rival's spiritual authority; formalize a claim over territory.
- **Vibes:** Authority, resentment, isolation.
- **Planar Behavior:** Bends other currents toward the user's will.
- **Reality Behavior:** Only works where the user's authority is already recognized.

### Threshold
- **Manifestation:** A faint tingling the user feels crossing any meaningful boundary.
- **Uses:** Mark a doorway, border, or boundary as significant and defended; sense when someone crosses a boundary they shouldn't; formalize a respected truce line.
- **Vibes:** Liminality, tension, relief.
- **Planar Behavior:** Boundaries here are far more porous than in reality.
- **Reality Behavior:** Only as respected as the authority backing it.

### Destiny
- **Manifestation:** A faint sense of "almost" surrounding a pivotal decision.
- **Uses:** Glimpse an alternate outcome that didn't happen; weigh several outcomes at once before committing; collapse an uncertain situation toward a chosen result.
- **Vibes:** Vertigo, hope, dread.
- **Planar Behavior:** Many outcomes coexist visibly at once.
- **Reality Behavior:** Collapses to exactly one outcome the instant it's observed.

### Foresight
- **Manifestation:** The user acts with certainty before conscious thought catches up.
- **Uses:** React to danger a half-beat faster than normal; sense a predator or prey's true nature at a glance; resist a Mimic- or Rumor-based deception on gut feeling.
- **Vibes:** Certainty, primal focus, unease.
- **Planar Behavior:** Foresight here is nearly always correct.
- **Reality Behavior:** Fallible, shaped by bias and fear.

### Fate
- **Manifestation:** A faint sense of inevitability around the user's choices.
- **Uses:** Tilt an uncertain outcome slightly; recognize a "destined" convergence before it happens; bind a promise so breaking it costs something real.
- **Vibes:** Dread, comfort, resentment.
- **Planar Behavior:** Threads visibly connect people and events.
- **Reality Behavior:** Only ever visible in hindsight.
