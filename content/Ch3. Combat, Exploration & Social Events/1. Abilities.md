# Abilities

Every character, regardless of Identity, element or build, draws from the same starting suite of actions and Building Blocks. You evolve and sculpt these over play into abilities named and shaped however you like in concert with your GM, sculpting your own Legacy ([Ch1. p4](obsidian://open?vault=TTRPG&file=Master%2F1.%20Version%202%2FCh1.%20Core%20Mechanics%2F4.%20Legacy%20%26%20Lineage)) over time.

**The names below are a loose guide, not a binding vocabulary.** Nothing stops you from calling your Fire Lash something entirely your own, these exist to help you understand what your character can do as you build them, and to give you and your GM a shared, teachable reference for what a given effect does mechanically.

## The Four Player-Facing Types

Every ability you'll ever use is tagged with exactly one of four words. You never need more than these four to understand what any ability in the game does:

- **Burst**: a single aimed moment, then gone.
- **Creation**: something left behind that persists.
- **Harmony**: a private shift in the practitioner, nothing external.
- **Sense**: an invisible act upon the world around you.

*(A deeper mechanical layer sits beneath these four, governing how the game itself resolves them. You and your GM will never need more than the four names above at the table to mechanically explain your actions.)*

Each type also carries its own Speed value, which combines with your chosen Approach to set how an action affects your position in next round's turn order. See **Turn Order** ([Ch3. p4](obsidian://open?vault=TTRPG&file=Master%2F1.%20Version%202%2FCh3.%20Combat%2C%20Exploration%20%26%20Social%20Events%2F4.%20Speed%20%26%20Turn%20Order)) for the full breakdown.

## Baseline Actions

These six actions are available to every character from the very first session, before you've built out a single unique named ability.

### Attack
A physical melee swing at a target near you.
- **Default:** roll against PDC/SDC to land the attack.
- Spend a Might Pip: enhance the impact, adding to your total roll.
- Must hit clean to damage 1 pip.

### Vent *(Burst/Creation-adjacent)*
A magical expelling of elemental power in a specific direction.
- **Default:** roll against PDC/SDC to vent at a target, or freely vent into the air beside you to lightly soak the environment in your element.
- Choose one: spend multiple Pips to multiply how much magic is expelled (power/density), **or** spend up to 3 Pips (1 pip for 1 band, 2 additional pips for 2 bands) to increase the size step in range.

### Defend
A physical or magical protection of yourself, temporarily raising your PDC.
- **Default:** step up the lowest die in your next defensive Pool roll before your next turn (being attacked, poisoned, avoiding a trap, etc.).
- Choose one: spend a Pip to further raise your PDC's Low or High Bar, 
	- **or** spend a Pip to step up a Pool die for the roll.

### Exposure *(Harmony-adjacent)*
A physical or magical active vulnerability of the self, temporarily lowering your PDC.
- **Default:** step down the lowest Pool die for a roll before your next turn.
- Choose one — spend a Pip to further reduce your PDC, 
	- **or** spend a Pip to step down a Pool die for the roll.

### Search *(Sense)*
An immediate physical or magical Pool roll to investigate an area, thing, or person.
- Spend up to a maximum of 2 Pips to step up up to 2 unique die for the roll.

### Interact
A quick manipulation of an object, mechanism, or the environment mid-encounter. 
	Example actions: Drawing a weapon, opening a door, pulling a lever, retrieving an item, shoving over a table.
- **Default:** no roll required for a simple, uncontested Interactions; it spends one of your 2 actions ([Ch3. p5](obsidian://open?vault=TTRPG&file=Master%2F1.%20Version%202%2FCh3.%20Combat%2C%20Exploration%20%26%20Social%20Events%2F5.%20Action%20Economy)). If the GM calls for uncertainty (a stuck door, a rigged mechanism), roll the relevant Skill's Pool against SDC instead.
- Spend a Pip: resolve the Interact as a free action instead, letting you still take two full actions that turn.

## The Ability Types, In Depth

Beyond the baseline actions, your growing Legacy draws from four families of Building Blocks, one per ability type. Each named Block below is a *shape*, meant to be paired with an element ([Ch4. p5](obsidian://open?vault=TTRPG&file=Master%2F1.%20Version%202%2FCh4.%20The%20Elements%20of%20the%20World%2F5.%20Natural%2C%20Bound%20%26%20Planar%20Elements)) to become an actual ability.

### Burst — damage and direct effect

Severity scales with the elemental effect and strength behind it.

1. **Element Lash** *(originates from the character, single target)*
	can evolve into **Spear** (for range), **Strike** (for power), or **Thrust** (for agility).

2. **Element Spike** *(originates from a point within range, single target)*
	can evolve into **Pillar** for an area effect.

3. **Element Inflict** *(originates from a point within range, single target)*
	a delayed or escalating effect rather than an immediate hit.

4. **Element Overload** *(originates from the target; can spend multiple resources)*
	can evolve into **Overwhelm** for a detrimental effect on the target.

5. **Element Ring** *(originates from the character)*
	vents elemental energy immediately around you; can be charged to last longer or vent more power quickly.

### Harmony — connection and balance

A shift altering the self, or those within your domain.

1. **Element Aura**
	emit an area of elemental power to enhance allies or dampen enemies; provides a concentrated dose of your element within the affected area.

2. **Element Body**
	harmonise your power with your physical self, empowering your Speed, Might, or PDC while active.

3. **Element Emission**
	bleed your element into your surroundings, slowly overwhelming other elements present.

4. **Element Link**
	connect to ambient element to gain element-specific benefits while active; passive versions can be built to reach for benefits of elements where your own presence runs thin.

### Creation — manifestation

Give your element direct, lasting form.

1. **Element Golem**
	directive-driven power made manifest.

2. **Element Mass**
	a concentrated ball or shape of elemental power made manifest. choose to plant it, detonate it, absorb it or any other purpose you can think of.

3. **Element Channel**
	meld your focus of creation into another ability, keeping it in this realm longer. Used in tandem with other Creation, Harmony, or Burst abilities.

4. **Element Bloom**
	create a shaped elemental construct: a tool, a weapon, or an object.

### Sense — perception

1. **Elemental Focus** *(passive, player- or GM-prompted)*
	bend your mind and body to meld with the elements around you; gauge the types of power present, their balance, and their impact on your own abilities if used.

2. **Far Sight** *(range scales with Power Tier, least as Nascent, greatest as Sovereign)*
	use keen eyes to observe your environment, distant creatures, or landmarks.

3. **Reach for the Elements**
	ascertain the elemental makeup of a specific being, creature, item, or place.

4. **Taste the Currents**
	grasp the flow of elements and the rough distance from you to the nearest nexus points. Can be specialised to hone in on farther nexus points of a specific element.

5. **Elemental Meld**
	pull your power into yourself, reducing your own ability to sense while also hiding yourself from the senses of others. While melded, you only ever roll 1 Pool die for any roll.

**Ability mutations as you grow your Sense kit:**
Each of the sense abilities can be mutated over time, tailored to your specific character and their goals. Below are some examples of how above Sense abilities could mutate.
- Adapting a Sense ability to notice when a creature, object, or power crosses a threshold.
- Gaining insight into another practitioner's weaknesses, based on their element, practitioner type, or signature.
- Detecting a *lack* of elemental presence in an area or item.
- Sensing Sprites, unbound Elementals, or Primes relative to your location, in Reality or Planar space alike (Planar speciailisation can gives unusually acute accuracy).
- Sensing how many resources (Pips) another practitioner or creature has left in one or more Pools.
- Perceiving through a created Golem's own senses.
- Sensing a target's SDC/PDC bars directly, always a difficult check to pull off.
- Sensing a thin point between Reality and the Planar realm.

## Elemental Variants

The Building Blocks above are shapes; the element you pair them with is what gives an ability its character. Below are worked examples, abilities built by pairing a Block with a specific element, to show how much a single shape can vary once an element is applied.
### Burst
**Lash:** 
	Fire Lash *(scorches through anything not fireproof; ignores soft cover)*
	Lightning Lash *(arcs to a second target if a conductor is in line of sight)*
	Force Lash *(pure kinetic shove; ignores elemental resistances entirely)*
	Sword Lash *(cuts the conceptually "uncuttable," including a tie or oath-bound effect)*

**Spike:** 
	Earth Spike *(erupts underfoot, anchoring the target on impact)* 
	Ice Spike *(impales and lingers, slowing via cold instead of vanishing)*
	Bone Spike *(erupts from nearby calcified material; unusable where none exists)*

**Inflict:** 
	Poison Inflict *(no immediate damage, weakens over successive rounds)*
	Rot Inflict *(organic targets only; decay accelerates each round it persists)*
	Curse Inflict *(trigger condition hidden, invisible until it fires)*

**Overload:** 
	Storm Overload *(pressure banks across turns, scaling with Pips spent before release)*
	Blood Overload *(costs Blood Pips instead of Might/Verve. your own vitality as fuel)*

**Ring:** 
	Sound Ring *(a deafening pulse staggers via disorientation, not damage)* · 
	Wind Ring *(a utility Burst: clears smoke, gas, or fog from the area instead of harming, Dashiell Or's)*

### Harmony

**Aura:** 
	Life Aura *(allies within regenerate Blood Pips slightly faster)*
	Death Aura *(enemy healing/regen effects are muffled within the zone)*
	Metal Aura *(allies' gear resists being disarmed or sundered)*
	Song Aura *(wordless emotion or orders carry to allies within, bypassing language)*

**Body:** 
	Water Body *(hard to pin or restrain; ignores some forced-movement effects)*
	Gravity Body *(PDC up, hard to knock down; Speed down while active)*
	Magma Body *(slow but devastating on contact, no ranged option)* 
	Static Body *(a burst of Speed triggers reactively when you take a hit)*

**Emission:** 
	Shadow Emission *(area darkens gradually, degrading enemy sight-based Sense checks)*
	Arcane Emission *(thins ambient magic, muffling other practitioners' Uses nearby)*
	Ash Emission *(fine ash coats the area, irritating and obscuring over time)*

**Link:** 
	Threshold Link *(passively aware the instant anything crosses a boundary in range)*
	Dream Link *(glimpse fragments of a sleeping linked target's dreams)*
	Sovereign Link *(allies within your declared domain gain a minor deference bonus)*

### Creation

**Golem:** 
	Wood Golem *(regrows/heals slowly each round left undamaged)* 
	Quake Golem *(generates tremors just by moving; impossible to approach quietly)* 
	Avalanche Golem *(weak opening hit, escalating power each consecutive turn, then collapses)*
	Frost Golem *(brittle shell shatters on first hit, reforms quickly after)*

**Mass:** 
	Light Mass *(illuminates the area; blinds anything that looks straight at it)*
	Destruction Mass *(grows more unstable and damaging each round left unreleased)* 
	Hollow Mass *(a null-core that suppresses other elemental effects in its radius)* 
	Steam Mass *(obscures vision on release, plus lingering scald)*

**Channel:** 
	Time Channel *(the linked ability's duration extends by one extra round)*
	Ritual Channel *(needs an uninterrupted turn to set up; linked effect becomes permanent until disrupted)*
	Echo Channel *(the linked ability faintly repeats once more, a round later)*

**Bloom:** 
	Chain Bloom *(forges binding restraints as the object rather than a weapon)*
	Crystal Bloom *(the forged object stores one charge of a spent ability for later release)*
	Glyph Bloom *(inscribes a ward-object instead of a tool; triggers on its condition)*

### Sense

**Elemental Focus:** 
	Omen Focus *(gauge shifts from "what's present" to a vague warning of "what's about to happen")*
	Planar Focus *(reads both Reality and the Elemental Plane simultaneously)*

**Far Sight:** 
	Far Foresight *(glimpses a distant scene a half-second before it happens)*
	Fog Sight *(reduced range, but works through low-light and obscured conditions)*

**Reach for the Elements:** 
	Blight Reach *(flags corrupted or diseased elemental makeup others would miss)*
	Destiny Reach *(reveals which element a target is trending toward bonding with, not their current makeup)*

**Taste the Currents:** 
	Tide Taste *(updates on a fixed rhythmic pulse rather than continuously, but unusually precise on that beat)*

**Elemental Meld:** 
	Silent Meld *(removes sound entirely rather than general presence; still visible, but truly silent)*
