# Legacy & Lineage

Everything your character can do — every ability, every elemental tie, every principle they hold to — belongs to your Legacy. If you find yourself creating a clan, building your legacy from an existing sects framework or another standing group then rather than having a Legacy, you will instead hold a Lineage.

## Legacy

Your **Legacy** is your own, personal collection: your Building Blocks (Ch3 §1), your elemental tie-ins, the tenets and morals you hold, and whatever cultural significance your Legacy carries.

Your Legacy isn't handed to you as a finished package. It grows over play — starting from the baseline actions and Building Blocks every character shares (Ch3 §1), then shaped further by the Identity you choose once you reach Kindled tier (Ch2 §7). Two characters can pick the same Identity and still end up with completely different Legacies, because Identity shapes *how* your kit behaves, not the full content of who you are.

A Legacy can be built from a single Building Block type alone, or spread evenly across all four — Burst, Creation, Harmony, Sense. Both are equally valid: a narrow specialist and a well-rounded generalist are two different flavors of legitimate, not a hierarchy of "better."

## Lineage

A **Lineage** is the same structure at cultural scale. When enough people converge on the same, or a closely related, Legacy; through shared teaching, shared origin, or shared circumstance - one person's individual path becomes a living tradition passed on to others.

This is the mechanism behind how a whole culture ends up with its own flavor of magic: the Elemental Suite's underlying physics (Ch4) is universal, but a Lineage is the cultural lens a whole people looks at that physics through.

## Building Your Character Around This

Keep Legacy and Lineage in mind as two different questions when you build your character:

- **Legacy** - what can *you* do, and why do you do it that way?
- **Lineage** - did you learn this from somewhere? Is there a tradition, a people, or a teacher behind your path - or are you forging something nobody's quite done this way before?

Neither answer needs to be dramatic. A quiet, personally-taught Legacy is just as honest as one passed down through a centuries-old Lineage.
