# Elemental Plane
The Elemental Plane is a second, **overlapping layer of the same reality**, not a place you travel to through a portal. Wherever you stand in Reality, the Elemental Plane is already there, layered underneath or alongside it. You can traverse between the two and locations around the world that resemble 'portals' do exist, though it's not uncommon that Practitioners within the influence of their Legacy's Element can blend themselves between realities, slipping in-between the two.

## What You Can Rely On
- **Prime proximity is always noticed**: You cannot be near a Prime Elemental and simply fail to register it.
- Every element in the world carries both a **Planar Behavior** and a **Reality Behavior** description. This means when an element acts differently on the Elemental Plane will differ to how it acts on the Reality/Physical plane. This is broken down and further examined for every element in the game (see the full table in [Ch4. p5](obsidian://open?vault=TTRPG&file=Master%2F1.%20Version%202%2FCh4.%20The%20Elements%20of%20the%20World%2F5.%20Natural%2C%20Bound%20%26%20Planar%20Elements)).

## What Remains Uncertain
- No comprehensive account exists of physically crossing between the two planes. The one point every source agrees on is the Planar element's own behavior: crossing is difficult, and always noticed. Beyond that, what plane-crossing actually entails is only ever implied by individual elements' behavior descriptions.
- Whether "always noticed", Prime proximity and plane-crossing alike, is a single, unified phenomenon, or two separate truths that simply happen to sound alike.
- How Metaphysical Weight's echo on the Elemental Plane ([Ch4. p4](obsidian://open?vault=TTRPG&file=Master%2F1.%20Version%202%2FCh4.%20The%20Elements%20of%20the%20World%2F4.%20Elemental%20Metaphysical%20Weight)) relates to a Prime's own presence there, should a sacred site and a Prime hotspot ever come to overlap.
