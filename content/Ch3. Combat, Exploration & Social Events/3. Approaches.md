# Approaches

If Skills answer "what are you doing," **Approaches** answer "how are you doing it." Approach is the term used for eight descriptors to flavor your actions with, arranged as four opposing pairs:

| Pair | Approach A  | Approach B |
| ---- | ----------- | ---------- |
| 1    | Flourish    | Wit        |
| 2    | Calculation | Passion    |
| 3    | Charm       | Resolve    |
| 4    | Guile       | Nerve      |

## Playing an Approach
The four opposing pairs give you built-in characterisation: leaning Flourish over Wit, or Passion over Calculation, says something about *how* your character solves problems, even when two characters use the identical Skill for an identical goal.

## Approach and Speed in Combat
Every Approach carries its own value on a 0-to-+6 scale. Whichever Approach you choose for an action adds that value onto your Speed for next round ([[4. Action Economy]]), alongside a value carried by the action's Ability Type ([[1. Abilities]]), the two add together.

## Speed & Approaches in Social and Exploration settings
Speed tallies how reactive your character is in each of the scenarios outlined by the skills tied to each Resource Pool while Approaches define how your speed will be impacted based on how you choose to approach an action.

| Approach    | Speed Value | Approach | Speed Value |
| ----------- | ----------- | -------- | ----------- |
| Flourish    | 2           | Wit      | 4           |
| Calculation | 1           | Passion  | 6           |
| Charm       | 2           | Resolve  | 5           |
| Guile       | 2           | Nerve    | 4           |
