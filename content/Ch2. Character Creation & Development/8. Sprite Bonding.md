# Sprite Bonding

Sprite Bonding is a measurable step of your character's growth with magic and the world itself. This is the moment, or slow process, by which a practitioner ties themselves to the world and their power in a metaphysical sense.

## Major Methods
The following methods produce the bulk of the world's Lineages and higher tier growth bonding with a sprite/sprites of your elemental selection.

1. **Sage's Codex**
	Magic as applied science; formal academic study until resonance is understood intellectually, then confirmed physically. The Elements are usually indifferent to the captivity and administration of their kin, though some specific Elements rebel against the imprisonment of themselves or their brethren. This is a slower, but more well-rounded method of practitioners with a Sense-leaning bias bonding with a Sprite. *(Yuki Marrow.)*

2. **Choir of Names**
	A communal rite: an entire cohort bonds to one or some shared elements together, with individual specialisation branching off afterwards.

3. **Still Water**
	Pure osmosis near a Prime or Boundless site. The bond is absorbed unconsciously over childhood, usually only noticed retroactively when a first ability manifests during emotional or physical extremity. *(Ren Talbot, Sable Quill.)*

## Solitary Methods
*(For wanderers and nomads — recognized, but hands-off.)*
4. **Drifting Accord**
	No fixed bonding. Practitioners carry portable totems and negotiate temporary, renewable accords with Sprites encountered while migrating. A Legacy built this way shifts in flavor as its people move. *(Nyx Cadence.)*

5. **Wandering Vigil** 
	A solitary rite: sent alone into unclaimed wilderness for a fixed period. Whichever Sprite finds you becomes your tie and has potential to mutate your chosen element.*(Dashiell Or.)*

## After First Bonding

Once bonded, an element doesn't sustain itself for free, see **Powering Your Legacy** ([[5. Powering your Legacy]]) for the ongoing practices that keep a bond fed.
