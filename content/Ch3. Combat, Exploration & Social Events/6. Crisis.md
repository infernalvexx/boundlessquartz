# Crisis

**Trigger:** all three of your Core Resource Pools — Blood, Might, and Verve — hit 0 Pips at the same time.

## While in Crisis

Roll a flat **3d4** every round you remain in Crisis. You die only if all three dice show **different values** — a flat 37.5% chance per round. This doesn't improve with tier: a Sovereign-tier character in Crisis dies at exactly the same rate as a Nascent one.

*Roll a 2, a 2, and a 4 — two dice match, so you survive the round. Roll a 1, a 3, and a 4 — all three different — and this is the round Crisis catches up to you.*

## Why It Works This Way

Crisis is meant to feel like a genuine coin-flip brush with death, regardless of how powerful your character has become. Your tier and build protect you from *reaching* Crisis in the first place — they do nothing to protect you once you're actually there. Getting yourself, or an ally, out of Crisis before the dice start falling is the entire point.
