# Identities
Once you reach **Kindled tier (Level 3)**, it's time to give your character a signature. Before that point, you're building your Legacy's first abilities out of the shared baseline Building Blocks ([Ch3. p1](obsidian://open?vault=TTRPG&file=Master%2F1.%20Version%202%2FCh3.%20Combat%2C%20Exploration%20%26%20Social%20Events%2F1.%20Abilities)) and getting comfortable with your character sheet.
Identities are deliberately gated behind the Third level so they don't overwhelm you before you've found your footing in understanding how dice rolling and scene progression functions.

## Choosing a Signature
Look through the list below and decide on the signature expression and style of character you want to grow into. This list is only a taste of what's possible in Boundless. Pick one outright, or bring an idea to your GM and work out something bespoke together. If a home-brewed signature doesn't end up feeling fun in play, you're encouraged to talk with your GM to refine the concept and make your fantasy a reality in Boundless, or you could try something else. If that still doesn't feel right, you always have the list below to fall back on.

| Identity                                              | Unique Resource/Mechanic                                                               | Identity Goal                                                                                |
| ----------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Feed your blade with magic to devastating effect      | **Charge Counters**: bank charge *on the weapon itself* to unleash whenever you please | Charge the blade through casting, cash it in through melee.                                  |
| Use your blood to defend your party                   | **Spend Blood Pips Directly**: Resource Protection is inverted                         | Be the one taking the hits.                                                                  |
| Not getting hit is the key to success                 | **Focus**: build Focus through being targeted by your enemies                          | Delay melee for as long as possible, punish those who chase you.                             |
| Cultivate a bond with your element, raise a companion | **Elemental Construct**: gain a tactical advantage through bonus resources             | Field and puppet a semi-independent second combatant.                                        |
| Keep away from enemies until you're ready to engage   | **Patience**: each action not spent harming an opponent builds Patience                | Unleash your Patient Hammer to devastating effect, or stand strong with your Patient Shield. |
| Awareness of your surroundings keeps everyone alive   | **Transfuse**: hitting clean steals a pip which you can give to an ally                | Target high-value enemies, restore in-need allies.                                           |
| Getting hurt is the plan                              | Less Blood Pips gives positive yet detrimental bonuses                                 | Challenge death to unleash the pinnacle of your strength.                                    |
| Intrinsically link yourself to another                | **Bond**: share resources with your chosen partner                                     | Provide utility and support in an unparalleled manner.                                       |

## Depth from Specialising your Character

Identity is a specialisation layer on top of your Legacy. 
Your Building Blocks and your Skills/Approaches already define what your character can do. Identity gives that foundation a signature hook: a resource or a reason your kit feels like *yours* and not just a list of abilities.
