# Birth and Death of the Elements

## Birth

Any element — **Natural or Bound, not restricted to Bound alone** — can be freshly born into the world. Birth is deliberately **GM-tracked, not framework-tracked**: there is no formal system dictating when or how a new element enters play. This is intentional, meant to encourage genuine table-level creativity rather than boxing a GM into a checklist.

A few things about Birth are still unsettled, worth knowing even though they won't change how you play day to day:
- Whether Birth has any mechanical trigger conditions at all, or is entirely GM/narrative fiat.
- Whether a newly-Born element always enters at Sprite stage (Ch4 §1), or can start anywhere on that scale.
- Whether Planar elements — tied specifically to inter-planar interaction — can be Born the same way Natural and Bound elements can, or whether that's excluded by their very definition.

## Death

Elements themselves don't "die" in a literal sense the way a creature does — but a region's *relationship* to an element absolutely can end, most concretely through **Metaphysical Weight succession** (Ch4 §3): when a powerful practitioner dies, or is replaced by one bonded to a different element, the old elemental character of a region can fade, blend, or be fully overwritten over time.

Whether a Sovereign-tier death is literally *the same event* as a Birth, just at the other end of the same mechanism, or two related-but-distinct systems that happen to produce similar outcomes, is not yet resolved — see Ch4 §3 for the fuller picture of what actually happens when regional elemental power changes hands.
