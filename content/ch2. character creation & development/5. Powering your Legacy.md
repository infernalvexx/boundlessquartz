# Powering Your Legacy

Once you've undergone your First Bonding (Ch2 §8) and formed a working tie to an element, that bond needs ongoing feeding — it doesn't just sit there on its own. **Sustaining Practices** are the repeatable menu of ways a practitioner keeps drawing their element in after the fact, generalizing the role a single ritual used to play alone.

Pick one, or several, that fit your character's personality and circumstances — these work as roleplaying hooks as much as mechanical ones. None of them are tied to any specific culture; treat this list as a menu to choose from, not a fixed assignment.

1. **Cradle Cycling** — slow, meditative breathing, drawing ambient elemental presence over time. The baseline every other practice varies from.
2. **Sparring Communion** — ritualized combat with a trusted partner; power drawn from controlled conflict.
3. **Treasure Consumption** — consuming an elemental-imbued item directly. Ties directly into Metaphysical Weight (Ch4 §3).
4. **Vigil Fasting** — abstaining from food or comfort near a Boundless or Prime site.
5. **Blood Tithe** — willingly spending Blood as an offering, converted into growth over time.
6. **Trial Combat** — formal duels or tournaments; the ongoing-practice cousin of the Planar Trial origin.
7. **Sprite Communion** — sustained, focused time near a specific Sprite, building an informal relationship.
8. **Dream Walking** — power drawn during sleep, especially near Dream-adjacent sites or elements.
9. **Song-Working** — communal chanting or singing, drawing power collectively.
10. **Scholarly Discourse** — debating and teaching elemental theory, refining understanding through argument.
11. **Craftbinding** — channeling power through the act of making something.
12. **Oathkeeping** — maintaining a long personal vow; incremental growth from unbroken continuity between sessions. *(Narrative/downtime pacing only — this doesn't conflict with the standing rule against Vow/Long-Investment mechanics in scene-level Pip economy, Ch3 §5.)*
13. **Wound-Tending** — caring for and healing others, power drawn reciprocally.
14. **Storm-Chasing** — seeking a Natural element's most extreme manifestation and surviving proximity to it.
15. **Debt-Calling** — calling in favors, converting social capital into elemental growth.
16. **Silent Fast** — abstaining from using your element entirely, letting pressure build before a deliberate release.
17. **Territorial Claiming** — marking and tending land tied to your element, power drawn from sustained stewardship.
18. **Elemental Trade** — bartering with practitioners of *other* elements, exchanging insight or power.
19. **Scarring Renewal** — reopening or renewing an old scar; the ongoing counterpart to the Scarring Rite origin.
20. **Nightwatch** — staying awake through a night significant to your element (a dusk, dawn, or eclipse vigil).
21. **Progeny Blessing** — an established practitioner formally passing power to a chosen successor or student. Ties directly into Lineage succession, and scholars of Metaphysical Weight (Ch4 §3) still argue over whether an heir blessed this way inherits any part of a predecessor's existing band, or simply begins radiating from zero like anyone else.
22. **Wild Hunt** — tracking and besting a wild creature strongly associated with your element.
23. **Confession Rite** — admitting a failure or weakness tied to your element, converting vulnerability into growth.
24. **Convergence Seeking** — traveling to where two elements' influence overlaps or conflicts, drawing power from the friction.
