# Character Sheet

Your character sheet is organized around the same shape as character creation itself — each block below feeds into the chapter of this handbook that explains it in full.

## Layout

1. **Identity header** — your name, your Highlight, your Flaw.
2. **Three Resource Pool cards** — Blood, Might, Verve (Ch2 §3): each shows your three die-slots and your current Pips.
3. **The nine-Skill grid** (Ch3 §2) — each Skill shown alongside which Pool it's permanently assigned to.
4. **Legacy & Abilities** (Ch1 §4, Ch3 §1) — your current Building Blocks, and your Identity once you've chosen one (Ch2 §7).
5. **Crisis tracker** (Ch3 §6).
