# Flight Bands

Flight in Project: Boundless is a hard combat gate, not flavor. There are three bands, not a simple flight toggle, and access is tied directly to your Power Scale tier (Ch2 §6):

| Band | Access |
|---|---|
| **Ground** | Everyone |
| **Low Sky** | Skyborn tier and above (Level 5+) |
| **High Sky** | Ascendant tier and above (Level 7+) |

## What a Band Mismatch Means

Fighting from a band your opponent can't reach gives you a mechanical edge — a flat step-up, or a PDC penalty applied to the grounded side.

*A Skyborn character fighting from Low Sky against a Ground-bound Nascent opponent gets a step-up on their attacks, while the grounded fighter takes a PDC penalty trying to land a hit back — difficult, not impossible.*

This is advantage, not an unhittability wall — a Ground-bound character fighting a Low Sky opponent isn't locked out of the fight entirely, just genuinely disadvantaged.
