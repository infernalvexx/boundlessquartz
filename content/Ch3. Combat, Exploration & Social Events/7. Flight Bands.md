# Flight Bands

Flight in Boundless is limited to those who have accrued enough power and garnered the attention of the Elements enough to bond a Sprite. There are three bands of verticality: Grounded, Lower Flight and Far Flight:

| Band            | Access                              |
| --------------- | ----------------------------------- |
| **Grounded**    | Everyone                            |
| **Low Flight**  | Skyborn tier and above (Level 5+)   |
| **High FLight** | Ascendant tier and above (Level 7+) |

## What a Flight Band Mismatch Means
Fighting from a band your opponent can't reach gives you a palpable advantage. A flat step-up, or a PDC penalty are applied to the grounded or lower flight banded side.

>*Dashiell Or (Level 5, Skyborn) fighting from Low Sky against a Ground-bound Nascent opponent like Ren Talbot gets a step-up on his attacks, while Ren takes a PDC penalty trying to land a hit back — difficult, not impossible.*

It's valuable to point out that this is an advantage, not a mechanical immunity. A Grounded character fighting a Low Sky opponent isn't locked out of the fight, just disadvantaged by a harder low or high SDC/PDC, or stepped down Die depending on what the GM rules.
