# Crisis
Crisis **Triggers** when any of your three Core Resource Pools: Blood, Might, or Verve, hit 0 Pips.

### While in Crisis
Roll a flat **3d4** every round you remain in Crisis. You earn a scar only if all three dice show **different values**, a flat 37.5% chance per round. This doesn't improve with tier: a Sovereign-tier character in Crisis receives Scars at exactly the same rate as a Nascent one.
When you have no pips remaining in a pool and you receive a scar, you may sacrifice a pip shell instead of receiving the scar.

Blood is a protected resource as previously mentioned ([Ch2. p3](obsidian://open?vault=TTRPG&file=Master%2F1.%20Version%202%2FCh2.%20Character%20Creation%20%26%20Development%2F3.%20Resource%20Pools)). With your Characters resources spent and their protections broken the last threads of their connection to the mortal plane are severed. If you lose all of your Pips and Shells in your Blood Pool, failing a Crisis roll will inevitably kill your character. 
If you have remaining Might or Verve Shells you may choose to lose one of those instead of a Blood Shell, preserving your Blood pool from being reduced to nothing.

### Why It Works This Way
Crisis is meant to feel like a slightly tilted in your favor coin-flip "brush with death", regardless of how powerful your character has become. Your tier and build protect you from *reaching* Crisis in the first place though they do nothing to protect you once you're at the Edge of Life.

### Getting out of a Crisis
There are few ways to get out of a Crisis. If you find yourself in this position, let us hope the dice are in your favor. The few ways are as follows:
1. Sacrifice an available Scene Die instead of losing a Pool Shell.
2. Rely on your allies to provide temporary pool shells to stave off death.
3. Complete the scene and recover missing resources.
4. Find safety within a Scene and rest, away from danger and with enough time to lick your wounds.
5. Items with unique purpose can assist with reduced Crisis interactions.
