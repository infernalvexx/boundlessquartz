# Dice & Rolls

Every check you make in Project: Boundless resolves the same way. Learn this once, and you know how to play the whole game.

## The Roll

**Roll three dice, add them together.** You are not counting successes, and you're not hunting for one big number - you're summing three dice drawn from whichever Resource Pool (Blood, Might, or Verve - [Ch2 p3](obsidian://open?vault=TTRPG&file=Master%2F1.%20Version%202%2FCh2.%20Character%20Creation%20%26%20Development%2F3.%20Resource%20Pools)) applies to what you're doing.


Each of your three dice sits in its own die-slot, and each slot has an independent size: **d4 → d6 → d8 → d10**. Because each slot grows on its own, a single Pool can carry mismatched dice - you might roll a d10, a d6, and a d4 together in the same Pool, depending on how your character has grown. This is deliberate: your capability broadens unevenly as you improve, rather than every roll gaining a flat bonus.

## Pool Level & Expected Roll

Add up the tier of your three dice in a Pool - a d4 counts as tier 0, up through a d10 at tier 3 - and you get that Pool's **Pool Level**, running 0 through 9.

> A d6, d8 & d8 equals a dice pool level of 5/9 as die 1 has upgraded once and die 2+3 have both upgraded twice.
> **When comparing your roll against a scene difficult you can estimate your Expected Roll by the average of each die in your pool added together: 3d4 = 7.5 average roll**

This plus any roll bonuses you may have gives you, and your GM, an easy way to gauge what a roll is likely to produce before the dice hit the table.

*Ren Talbot, a Level 1 quarryhand barely a week into his First Bonding, has a Might Pool of three plain d4s - Pool Level 0, an Expected Roll around 7.5. Rolling a 2, a 3, and a 1 gives a 6: a touch below expected. That's normal - Expected Roll is an average, not a guarantee.*

## Cross-Pool Checks

If the fiction supports it, you can pull dice from more than one Pool for a single check - a desperate shove that's part Might, part Verve-fueled adrenaline, for instance. Talk to your GM when a situation genuinely straddles two Pools (See Blood, Might, or Verve - [Ch2 p3](obsidian://open?vault=TTRPG&file=Master%2F1.%20Version%202%2FCh2.%20Character%20Creation%20%26%20Development%2F3.%20Resource%20Pools) for more details on each pool and its purpose).
