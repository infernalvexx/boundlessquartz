# Elemental Metaphysical Weight

At **Ascendant tier (Level 7–8)** and intensifying through **Sovereign (Level 9–10)**, a practitioner begins passively radiating their bonded element in an expanding band around themselves. This is the mechanism by which a person, not just a place, becomes a living center of elemental gravity.

>*Thane Rourke, a Level 10 Magma-bonded Sovereign, carries this visage: a passive Magma band radiates outward from him, molten and heavy, slow to wane once established, growing further with time and exertion of power. 
>Cass Aurelian, Sovereign-bonded and Level 9, carries the same mechanism in a very different register: her band mutes other elements nearby and lends her own Sovereign-flavor to her surroundings.*

## The Core of this Weight
- The band **muffles rather than removes** other elements within it, non-native elements work noticeably worse nearby, though not 'not-at-all'.
- Naturally-occurring resources and treasures forming within the band lean toward the resident element, and can be consumed by other characters (**Treasure Consumption**, [Ch2. p5](obsidian://open?vault=TTRPG&file=Master%2F1.%20Version%202%2FCh2.%20Character%20Creation%20%26%20Development%2F5.%20Powering%20your%20Legacy)) to grow their own power.
- This is self-reinforcing: a powerful Legacy or Lineage's territory keeps producing more of exactly what made it powerful in the first place.
## Mechanical Shape
- The band's radius scales **exponentially per individual Level**. A Level 10 Sovereign's band is dramatically larger than a Level 9's. Your GM sets the precise multiplier at the table; the exponential *shape* of the curve is the fixed part.
- Non-native elemental Uses inside the band take a step-down hot to some of their Die; the resident element's own Uses get a step-up if they have established their imprint on the land around them.
- Treasures generated within the band automatically carry a bonus Use tied to the resident element.
Power concentration is followed qualitatively, narrated through where Ascendant and Sovereign-tier Elementalists are and have been.

## Death and Succession
A **Sovereign-tier death births a sacred site**. A major, lasting impact on the Reality plane, with a lesser echo on the Elemental Plane or vice versa depending on the Element in question. New Boundless Elemental sites are often recorded to have been born from these such events.
**Absent death**. A Influence from a strong Practitioner wanes slowly once its source moves on, unless a comparable-tier Practitioner moves in, in which case the influence persists and re-centers on them. Presence alone is enough to hold or transfer a region.
**Cross-element succession:** If a new Practitioner of similar power but a *different* element moves into a region still carrying a prior resident's Influence, the two **blend for a transitional period**, then the newcomer's element can **partially** or **fully replace** the old one over a long span. In some rare cases the Influence becomes shared and a new melding on the two Elements bleeds into the world. A region known today for Water may still carry old Fire-flavored ruins from a Fire legend a generation prior for example.
