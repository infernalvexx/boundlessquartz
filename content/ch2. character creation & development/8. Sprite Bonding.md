# Sprite Bonding

Sprite Bonding is your character's growth with magic and the world itself. This is the moment, or slow process, by which a practitioner first ties themselves to the world and their power in a metaphysical sense. How much cultural institution stands behind that moment says a lot about who your character is and where they come from.

## Major Methods

The following methods produce the bulk of the world's Lineages.

1. **Planar Trial of the Elements** — a formal, dangerous proving ground; *how* a candidate survives, not just whether they do, determines which element tied to the practitioners legacy answers. Fits institutional, hierarchical cultures — the soft-canon origin for meritocratic, trial-tested practitioners. *(Corvin Ashe, Cass Aurelian.)*

2. **Scarring Rite** — power is only treated as real if it costs something visible. A deliberate wound or trial-by-combat forces an early, unstable bond, biased toward Blood/Sacrifice and other pain-adjacent elements. *(Petra Vane, Baldric Fenn, Thane Rourke.)*

3. **Sage's Codex** — magic as applied science; formal academic study until resonance is understood intellectually, then confirmed physically. The Elements are usually indifferent to the captivity and administration of their kin, though some specific ELements rebel against the imprisonment. Slow, but produces well-rounded practitioners with a Sense-leaning bias. *(Yuki Marrow.)*

4. **Choir of Names** — a communal rite: an entire cohort bonds to one or some shared elements together, with individual specialization branching off afterward. The clearest case of a Lineage coming *before* an individual Legacy. Fits Council-of-Elders or Hive-Collective style cultures. *(Wren Halloway, Ilyria Vask.)*

5. **Still Water** — pure environmental osmosis near a Prime or Boundless site. The bond is absorbed unconsciously over childhood, usually only noticed retroactively when a first ability manifests during real emotional extremity. *(Ren Talbot, Sable Quill.)*

## Solitary Methods

*(For wanderers and nomads — recognized, but hands-off.)*

6. **Drifting Accord** — no fixed bonding. Practitioners carry portable totems and negotiate temporary, renewable accords with Sprites encountered while migrating. A Legacy built this way shifts in flavor as its people move. *(Nyx Cadence.)*
7. **Wandering Vigil** — a solitary rite: sent alone into unclaimed wilderness for a fixed period. Whichever Sprite finds you becomes your tie. This works equally well as a sanctioned cultural practice, or as something a lone straggler might do with no cultural backing at all. *(Dashiell Or.)*

## After First Bonding

Once bonded, an element doesn't sustain itself for free — see **Powering Your Legacy** (Ch2 §5) for the ongoing practices that keep a bond fed.
