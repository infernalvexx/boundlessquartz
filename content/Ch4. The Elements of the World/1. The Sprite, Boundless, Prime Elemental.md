# The Sprite / Boundless / Prime Elemental

Every element in the world follows the same three-stage hierarchy, uniform in shape no matter which element it is.

| Stage | What It Is |
|---|---|
| **Sprite** | The entry stage — the smallest, weakest manifestation of an element. This is the stage tied to Sprite Bonding (Ch2 §8). |
| **Boundless Elemental** | The mid stage — a "site"-scale manifestation. This is the model an Ascendant/Sovereign-tier practitioner starts to resemble as their Metaphysical Weight (Ch4 §3) grows. |
| **Prime Elemental** | The apex stage. Prime proximity is always noticed — hotspots never need a tectonic or narrative justification to exist; the world simply knows when one is near. |

## What Isn't Pinned Down Yet

A few genuine open questions sit underneath this hierarchy, worth knowing about even though they don't change how you play day to day:

- What mechanically distinguishes a Sprite from a Boundless Elemental from a Prime, beyond raw scale and intensity — whether that's ever a formal stat block, or stays narrative-only the way Metaphysical Weight does.
- Whether every element has confirmed examples at all three stages, or whether some — particularly Bound and Planar elements — skip a stage entirely.
- How two different elements' Sprites, Boundless Elementals, or Primes interact when they end up in proximity to one another.
- The exact relationship between this hierarchy and the Birth mechanic (Ch4 §2) — does a newly Born element always start at Sprite stage and grow, or can it be born directly at a larger scale?
