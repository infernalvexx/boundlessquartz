# Practitioners: Cast / Wield / Shape

Every practitioner of magic falls into one of three types which infer the Resource Pool (See [Ch2. p3](obsidian://open?vault=TTRPG&file=Master%2F1.%20Version%202%2FCh2.%20Character%20Creation%20%26%20Development%2F3.%20Resource%20Pools)) they lean on. This triad is your character's flavor layer for *how* they express power - caster, martial, or a hybrid of the two - independent of which Pool your individual Skills happen to be assigned to.

| Type                | Lean                          | Default Speed | Seen on                                                         |
| ------------------- | ----------------------------- | ------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Cast / Caster**   | Verve-heavy                   | 2             | Yuki Marrow, Wren Halloway, Cass Aurelian                       |
| **Wield / Wielder** | Might-heavy                   | 4             | Ren Talbot, Petra Vane, Dashiell Or, Baldric Fenn, Thane Rourke |
| **Shape / Shaper**  | Verve and Might roughly equal | 3             | Corvin Ashe, Nyx Cadence, Ilyria Vask                           |

Blood sits outside this axis entirely. Blood is your survival and health, not a "how you display your power" descriptor, so it plays no part in deciding whether you're a Caster, Wielder, or Shaper.

## Why It Matters Beyond Flavor

Practitioner Type decides which Pool you roll for your Speed check at the top of combat ([Ch3. p4](obsidian://open?vault=TTRPG&file=Master%2F1.%20Version%202%2FCh3.%20Combat%2C%20Exploration%20%26%20Social%20Events%2F4.%20Turn%20Order)) as well as impacting the shape of your characters growth:
- **Casters** generally roll Verve, gain less speed but gain the most pool pip bonuses.
- **Wielders** generally roll Might, gain the most speed but gain the least pool pip bonuses.
- **Shapers choose** which of the two to roll as it suits them and have average growth between the three practitioner types.
