# Character Sheet

Your character sheet is organised around the same shape as character creation itself. Each block below feeds into the segments throughout this handbook chapter that explains each mention in full.

## Layout

1. **Character header** - your character name, level and player name
2. **Personal Links** - Legacy, Lineage, Elements and Identity
3. **Type of Character** - Practitioner, Narrative Title & Experiences (See Ch2. p2, p4 & p6)
4. **The Three Resource Pools** - Blood, Might, Verve (See Ch2. p3): each shows your three die-slots, your current Pips for that pool and your pools Skills (See Ch3. p2)
5. **Speed & Approaches** - Shows your speed per resource & Approaches that affect Speed when you take action (See Ch3. p3)
